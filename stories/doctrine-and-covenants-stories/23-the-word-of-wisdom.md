The Lord’s law of health

February 1833

---

![A meeting of the School of the Prophets.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/n71mshzjxswhjelllpwm6kx0iyrbd4x4ai1mgabw/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph Smith invited a few men in the Church to have meetings with him on the top floor of Newel and Ann Whitney’s store. There they learned the gospel and other subjects, and they prayed as a group. They called these meetings the School of the Prophets.

[Doctrine and Covenants 88:117–137](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/88?lang=eng&id=p117-p137#p117); Saints, [1:166](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p38-p39#p38)

---

![Emma Smith cleaning up after the meeting.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/pxk6r92aput7ujh269tzrf0g3ycgl997zynz5dng/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Many of the men smoked pipes and chewed tobacco during the lessons. The smoke made the air cloudy, and the tobacco made a mess on the floors. After the meetings, Emma Smith went upstairs to clean the room. It was very dirty!

Saints, [1:167](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p43#p43)

---

![Emma talking to Joseph.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/evi0khsssrddu9qsy3g0fgxtvpnrzm4brb5fg30l/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Emma scrubbed and scrubbed the floors, but the tobacco stains would not come out. She talked to Joseph about the mess.

Saints, [1:167](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p43-p44#p43)

---

![Joseph listening to Emma.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/magnzvdhmxj9nys6gvhgeyy1kldewh73wtv48mac/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph listened to Emma. Hearing her made him wonder how the Lord felt about tobacco.

Saints, [1:167](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p44#p44)

---

![Joseph praying for guidance.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/rdyw7uy2357eohmfq824ooil4mh2le7ao6ut4rpn/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

So Joseph asked the Lord about tobacco. The Lord told Joseph that he had a Word of Wisdom for the Saints.

[Doctrine and Covenants 89:1–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/89?lang=eng&id=p1-p9#p1); Saints, [1:167](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p46#p46)

---

![Joseph and Emma talking about the revelation he has received.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/r0vpxa5izgl9gonnjab2170j8g41rgmdyz71nn8j/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord said the Saints should not smoke, use tobacco, or drink alcohol or “hot drinks,” which means coffee and tea.

[Doctrine and Covenants 89:1–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/89?lang=eng&id=p1-p9#p1); Saints, [1:167](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p46#p46)

---

![Joseph and Emma sharing a meal with others.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/iffthdao26f4nzmu5vee6oisht5msmp7pywh6s3q/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord also taught the Saints that grains and fruit are good to eat. He also said they could sometimes eat meat.

[Doctrine and Covenants 89:10–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/89?lang=eng&id=p10-p17#p10); Saints, [1:168](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p47#p47)

---

![Joseph sharing the Word of Wisdom with the School of the Prophets.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ret6jmdzk4fa7x9oken9s21bvwb6yys1qtcshufw/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord told Joseph that the Saints who chose to obey His Word of Wisdom would be protected. The Lord promised to bless them with health, knowledge, and strength. Joseph told the men in the School of the Prophets about the Lord’s Word of Wisdom.

[Doctrine and Covenants 89:18–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/89?lang=eng&id=p18-p21#p18); Saints, [1:168](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p47#p47)

---

![Men throwing their tobacco and smoking pipes into the fire.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/59mxl6oc1c58szuscbil8rq2rnlk6zocj9nhicqf/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The men in the room threw their tobacco and their pipes into the fire to show that they wanted to obey the Lord’s Word of Wisdom.

Saints, [1:168](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/15-holy-places?lang=eng&id=p49#p49)
