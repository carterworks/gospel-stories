Walking with faith in the Lord

1841–1843

---

![Jane Manning being baptized.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/xtztxpsjnqkzei99nt30bj65vawxoxmy8e8err9l/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jane Manning and her family lived in a place where some people did not like them and treated them poorly because of the color of their skin. One day, Jane heard a missionary teaching. She knew his message was true. The very next Sunday, she was baptized.

Saints, [1:500–501](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p34-p35#p34)

---

![Jane and her family beginning their journey to Nauvoo.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/iy398ob18mg1r8e9p2a0kseghdgfb388ulb0v6x7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jane’s family joined the Church too. The Lord wanted the Saints to gather together in Nauvoo. Jane loved the Lord. She and her family wanted to be with the other Saints. So they began their journey to Nauvoo.

[Doctrine and Covenants 124:25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p25#p25); Saints, [1:501](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p36#p36)

---

![Jane and her family are refused travel on the boat.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/vvvwszhq9v6sdsyz18n0jyje2cauj8y8wr90gko8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

For part of their trip, they traveled by boat. Most people on the boat could pay for the ride at the end of the trip. But some men told Jane’s family they had to pay at the beginning. They did not have enough money. The men made them get off the boat, and it left without them.

Saints, [1:501](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p37-p40#p37)

---

![Jane and her family walking to Nauvoo.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7to268gvp9cv4r8rwxrxpow95niyogk1q9w7820m/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jane’s family still had to travel 800 miles, or 1,300 kilometers. They had to cross a deep stream without a bridge. They had to sleep outside. The weather was often very cold. But Jane’s family was determined to reach Nauvoo. They cheered themselves up by singing as they walked.

Saints, [1:501](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p40#p40), [505–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p60-p61#p60)

---

![Jane caring for a family member.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/waqmia1x1ihrwl75ko5uoil0ziu0hjil5mt2zmlm/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jane and her family walked and walked until their shoes wore out and their feet got hurt. But when they prayed to God for help, He healed their feet.

Saints, [1:505](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p59#p59)

---

![Jane and her family helping a sick child.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6cm0yuejtieuyfah41p9nd25r2i3iavhac1i3010/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

They helped the people they met along their journey. They even helped heal a sick child because of their faith.

Saints, [1:505–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p60#p60)

---

![Emma and Joseph welcome Jane into their home.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/vdtzgmrg2ft7cl3gm836o6bngwqkzgeme4mdn6lr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Finally, Jane and her family reached Nauvoo! They were tired and needed a place to stay. So they went to Emma and Joseph’s house. When Joseph heard all they had been through, he told Jane, “God bless you. You are among friends now.” Joseph and Emma invited Jane to live with them.

Saints, [1:506](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/41-god-must-be-the-judge?lang=eng&id=p57-p67#p57)
