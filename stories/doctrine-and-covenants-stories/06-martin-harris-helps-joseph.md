Learning to trust the Lord

1828–1829

---

![Martin Harris and his wife greet Joseph and Emma Smith.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0e4fc6e11c5211efa3b2eeeeac1e3674adc17c54/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, while Joseph and Emma were living in Pennsylvania, a friend named Martin Harris came to visit them. Martin owned a large farm in New York. He knew about Joseph’s work with the gold plates. He had helped Joseph before, and now he wanted to know if there was more he could do to help.

Saints, [1:49](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p33-p37#p33)

---

![A close-up illustration of Martin Harris. He is standing in the kitchen of the home of Joseph and Emma Smith.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0e56a4b11c5211ef80edeeeeac1e0b5cc9c11b3b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph and Emma were thankful. Many people thought Joseph was lying. It was good to have a friend like Martin who believed the gold plates were real.

Saints, [1:49](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p33-p37#p33)

---

![Joseph and Emma Smith standing inside their home with Martin Harris. Joseph and Martin are holding pages of a manuscript.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0e7e78031c5211ef9fa6eeeeac1e34dc603cecb8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

As Joseph translated the plates, Martin wrote down what he said. Martin was excited to be helping the Lord with this great work.

Saints, [1:50](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p38-p41#p38)

---

![Martin and Lucy Harris arguing in front of their horse-drawn carriage.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0ead02121c5211efb77eeeeeac1e17da9bbbd33c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Martin’s wife, Lucy, was not happy. She did not believe Joseph really had gold plates. She thought Joseph was tricking Martin. She wanted Martin to come back to New York and stop helping Joseph.

Saints, [1:49–50](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p33-p41#p33)

---

![Joseph Smith holding a page of a manuscript. Martin Harris is asking Joseph to borrow the manuscript to show to his wife.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0f0fe2921c5211efa3b2eeeeac1e3674ff50775d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But Martin kept working with Joseph. He thought that if Lucy could read what they were working on, she would believe too. He asked Joseph if he could take the pages they had translated to New York to show his wife. Joseph said that he would pray and ask God.

Saints, [1:50–51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p38-p46#p38)

---

![Joseph Smith praying to ask God if Martin Harris can borrow the manuscript.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0f2ae4a11c5211ef9b9feeeeac1e5a2d7f1a53b5/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

God told Joseph not to let Martin take the pages. Martin asked Joseph to pray again. Joseph prayed and got the same answer. But Martin really wanted to show his wife the pages, and Joseph wanted to help his friend. Joseph prayed a third time. This time God told Joseph he could decide what to do.

Saints, [1:51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p42-p46#p42)

---

![Martin Harris holding the pages of the manuscript. Joseph Smith in the background, looking worried.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0f5354331c5211ef92d4eeeeac1ef2a5c6ca770e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph told Martin that he could take the pages to New York, but he could show them only to certain family members. And he needed to bring the pages back in two weeks. Martin agreed and left with the pages.

Saints, [1:51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p42-p46#p42)

---

![Joseph Smith kneeling at the grave of his deceased baby.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0f9602821c5211ef8fd0eeeeac1ed92e162dc66f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

While Martin was gone, a baby was born to Joseph and Emma. But the baby died, and Emma became sick. Emma and Joseph were very sad.

Saints, [1:51–52](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p42-p53#p42)

---

![Joseph Smith embracing Emma.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0fb682d21c5211ef9b9feeeeac1e5a2da357f7c2/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph worried about Emma. He also worried about Martin. The two weeks had passed, and Martin had not come back. Emma was worried too.

Saints, [1:52](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p47-p52#p47)

---

![Martin Harris sitting at a table, talking to Joseph Smith and his parents.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/104ad3931c5211ef9b9feeeeac1e5a2de09edefc/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Emma told Joseph to go to New York to look for Martin. Joseph met Martin at his parents’ home. When Joseph asked Martin about the pages, Martin was very sad. He said the pages were lost. He had looked everywhere but could not find them.

Saints, [1:52–53](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p48-p61#p48)

---

![Emma Smith embracing Joseph Smith.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1057cbe11c5211ef839ceeeeac1ebda07c637379/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph became very sad and afraid. He knew he was wrong to let Martin take the pages. He went back home and told Emma what happened. The angel Moroni came and took back the gold plates. He said if Joseph was humble and repented, he could have the plates back and translate again.

[Doctrine and Covenants 3:5–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/3?lang=eng&id=p5-p11#p5); [10:1–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/10?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1); Saints, [1:53–54](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/05-all-is-lost?lang=eng&id=p54-p61#p54)

---

![Joseph Smith kneeling at his bedside, asking God for forgiveness.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/107195771c5211ef8ab1eeeeac1ea09db57e8dd3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

For many weeks, Joseph thought about what he did wrong. He prayed and asked for forgiveness. God did forgive him. He told Joseph that the pages had been stolen by people who were trying to stop God’s work. But no one can stop God’s work. God had a plan to keep His work going.

[Doctrine and Covenants 3:1–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/3?lang=eng&id=p1-p10#p1); Saints, [1:54–55](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/06-the-gift-and-power-of-god?lang=eng&id=p1-p7#p1)

---

![Joseph and Emma Smith talking with Martin Harris. Joseph is shaking Martin Harris’s hand.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/10a995631c5211ef8fd0eeeeac1ed92eed6b01eb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Moroni gave the plates back to Joseph. God told Joseph and Martin that if they were humble and trusted Him, they could keep helping with His work in many ways. They could help bring the Book of Mormon to the world. This book would help people everywhere repent and believe in Jesus Christ.

[Doctrine and Covenants 3:16–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/3?lang=eng&id=p16-p20#p16); [5:21–35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/5?lang=eng&id=p21-p35#p21); Saints, [1:56–57](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/06-the-gift-and-power-of-god?lang=eng&id=p8-p16#p8)
