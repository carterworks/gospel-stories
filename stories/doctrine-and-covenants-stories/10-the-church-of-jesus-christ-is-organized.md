A joyful Church meeting

April 1830

---

![Joseph Smith welcoming people to the Whitmer home for the first meeting of the Church.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/tzjnnwu4pepytghzpwkgsyolbd7hmcyky72btb2l/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Book of Mormon was now printed, and God’s priesthood power was on the earth again. The Lord told Joseph Smith it was time to organize the Church of Jesus Christ.

Saints, [1:84](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/08-rise-of-the-church-of-christ?lang=eng&id=p47-p50#p47)

---

![People raise their hands to sustain Joseph and Oliver as leaders of the Church.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4d9dr4iadt5ytlr1eohxgaclxy4eqhk8h1vrmt8d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

On April 6, 1830, about 40 women and men came to the Whitmer family’s home to organize the Church. The Lord had called Joseph and Oliver to lead His Church. Joseph and Oliver asked the people to raise their hands to show they sustained them as their leaders.

[Doctrine and Covenants 20:1–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/20?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1); Saints, [1:84](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/08-rise-of-the-church-of-christ?lang=eng&id=p47-p50#p47)

---

![New members of the Church were happy to take the sacrament.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/gqpzrsd7goek95yojls5t1s6rnrrdb3lfovifvnq/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Next, Joseph and Oliver blessed the sacrament. They gave it to people to help them remember Jesus Christ. Joseph and Oliver then put their hands on the heads of people who had been baptized. They confirmed them members of the Church and gave them the gift of the Holy Ghost.

[Doctrine and Covenants 20:75–79](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/20?lang=eng&id=p75-p79#p75); Saints, [1:85](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/08-rise-of-the-church-of-christ?lang=eng&id=p50-p54#p50)

---

![Joseph teaching new members of the Church.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/zwjzgfruxx5zcnreoagywig9z9thnxx9l9nfrcoo/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord gave Joseph a message for the new Church. He told them to write down what happened in the Church. He said that Joseph Smith was His prophet. He promised Church members that if they followed the commandments He gave Joseph, He would bless them. The devil would not have power over them.

[Doctrine and Covenants 21:1–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/21?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1); Saints, [1:85](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/08-rise-of-the-church-of-christ?lang=eng&id=p50-p54#p50)

---

![Joseph Smith and his father hugging each other.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ttpzr9nzjtxkzdrwnsgdfxfc2cydkzq6iohrtyou/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The people were so happy to have the Church of Jesus Christ! After the meeting, more people were baptized, including Joseph’s mother and father. When his father came out of the water, Joseph gave him a big hug. “I have lived to see my father baptized into the true church of Jesus Christ!” he said.

Saints, [1:86](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/08-rise-of-the-church-of-christ?lang=eng&id=p55-p59#p55)

---

![Joseph felt very thankful to help restore the Church.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bnzrpexdwteokc2gjpnq82olsd1chztpp8bkkklf/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Later that night, Joseph went to the woods to be alone. His heart was full of joy. It had been 10 years since he first saw Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. So much had happened since then. Joseph was very thankful he could help restore, or bring back, Jesus Christ’s Church!

Saints, [1:86](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/08-rise-of-the-church-of-christ?lang=eng&id=p55-p59#p55)
