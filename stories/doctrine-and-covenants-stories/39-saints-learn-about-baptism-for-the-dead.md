Hope for family members who were not baptized

August–October 1840

---

![Jane Neyman at a funeral where Joseph Smith is speaking.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ziywqcxalk720ixvxgb3ryh75syzvz212ydbclcm/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jane Neyman lived in Nauvoo. She went to a funeral where Joseph Smith was speaking. The funeral made Jane think about her son Cyrus. He had died and wasn’t baptized. Jane worried about Cyrus. She knew that we have to be baptized to live with Heavenly Father.

Saints, [1:421](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/35-a-beautiful-place?lang=eng&id=p28-p29#p28)

---

![Joseph kneeling in front of Alvin Smith’s grave.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/me7smbj6m42u7hpry8qvke5rbbyrhnq37z7ddz6s/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph knew how she felt. He used to wonder the same thing about his brother Alvin, who had also died without being baptized.

Saints, [1:421](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/35-a-beautiful-place?lang=eng&id=p29#p29)

---

![Joseph introduces the teaching of baptism for the dead.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bdiot06n4a4etbkb95m7ppli9e8c8lan6ku7v74e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

At the funeral, Joseph shared something God had taught him. He said that when someone has died without being baptized, we can be baptized for them.

Saints, [1:421](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/35-a-beautiful-place?lang=eng&id=p30-p31#p30)

---

![Jane being baptized for her son.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/d2qt8rc6uno9uxxnnhilz6t5o15e7xgy8wlhxr85/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jane was so happy to hear this! Soon after the funeral, she went to the river with an elder of the Church. He baptized her for Cyrus.

Saints, [1:421](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/35-a-beautiful-place?lang=eng&id=p32#p32)

---

![Joseph teaching more Saints about baptism for the dead.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/lrl1f2mw9vd3rmx7sepidxs9yiuuhc3a0uo7t4fc/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A few weeks later, Joseph taught the Saints more about baptism for the dead. He said that many people who had died wanted to be baptized. They wanted to follow Jesus Christ. They were waiting and hoping for their family members who were still alive to be baptized for them.

[Doctrine and Covenants 128:15–19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/128?lang=eng&id=p15-p19#p15); Saints, [1:423](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/35-a-beautiful-place?lang=eng&id=p44#p44)

---

![Saints being baptized for the dead.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7yc7zqn90kf11k7ug0ja9nrhxwricia4omhuzppn/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Saints ran to the river right away. They were baptized for people they loved. Hyrum Smith was baptized for Alvin. Later, the Lord taught that baptism for the dead should be done in the temple, someone should keep a record of each baptism, and men should be baptized for men and women for women.

[Doctrine and Covenants 124:29–32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/124?lang=eng&id=p29-p32#p29); [127:5–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/127?lang=eng&id=p5-p12#p5); [128:1–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/128?lang=eng&id=p1-p9#p1); Saints, [1:424](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/35-a-beautiful-place?lang=eng&id=p45#p45)
