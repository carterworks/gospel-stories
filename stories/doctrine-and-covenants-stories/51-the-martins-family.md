Waiting for the Lord’s blessings

April 1972–November 1978

---

![Helvécio Martins driving home from work.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/djq12dknwk68x9qxswktzvkb9ygqfp3xg4lwp829/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Helvécio Martins was driving home from work in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. The road was so full of cars that none of them could move. Helvécio thought about his life. He had a good job. He loved his wife, Rudá, and his two children, Marcus and Marisa. But he felt like something was missing.

Saints, [4:229–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/14-different-now?lang=eng&id=p33-p37#p33)

---

![Helvécio kneeling in prayer.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fbtzox7995p6li888275dgtd3bvsed98v3cswvba/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Helvécio got out of the car and began to pray. “My God,” he said, “I know you are there someplace, but I don’t know where.” He told Heavenly Father that his family was searching for something, and they needed His help. Then Helvécio got back in his car and drove home.

Saints, [4:230](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/14-different-now?lang=eng&id=p38-p39#p38)

---

![The Martins greeting Latter-day Saint missionaries.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/j8q1k286s9prwdyyx5v4hrtfyop66wcvozv2dfkw/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Later, the Lord sent missionaries from the United States. They visited the Martins family. Helvécio noticed that they brought a peaceful spirit to his home. He knew that, at the time, people with dark skin were often not treated well in the United States. He asked, “How does your religion treat Blacks?”

Saints, [4:230–31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/14-different-now?lang=eng&id=p43-p46#p43)

---

![The missionaries explaining temple and priesthood restrictions to the Martins.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/47eo7z9otqa0so668x22b2v9hn77x9b5w0hefesr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The missionaries explained that all of God’s children could be baptized. But at that time, Black people with ancestors from Africa could not hold the priesthood or receive most temple blessings. Helvécio and Rudá had many more questions. The missionaries did their best to answer them.

Saints, [4:231](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/14-different-now?lang=eng&id=p47-p50#p47)

---

![Church President David O. McKay kneeling in prayer.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/wpgrfprx43pw5wsenkzgo3sinozalack3zptjlgj/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

For many years, prophets had prayed to know when priesthood and temple blessings could be given to all people.

Saints, [4:71](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-1/05-no-power-on-earth?lang=eng&id=p24-p26#p24)

---

![The Martins greeting people at a Church meeting.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6pu8jj9nt6qioxh69bhbgnk1j84alnqfp8r4cw07/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Helvécio and Rudá decided to try going to church. The Saints there were very loving and kind. The Martins family liked what they learned at church.

Saints, [4:231–32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/14-different-now?lang=eng&id=p51#p51)

---

![The Martins family deciding to be baptized.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0qirj13qt9uhsd1fxtkjhf2ondhijj4a3yg6eofy/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, on the way home from church, Marcus told his family that he noticed they seemed happier. “I know what is causing it,” Marcus said. “The gospel of Jesus Christ.” The rest of the family knew Marcus was right. They decided to be baptized and confirmed.

Saints, [4:232](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/14-different-now?lang=eng&id=p52-p54#p52)

---

![Helvécio and Rudá visiting the site of the São Paulo Brazil Temple.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ugwvnsxb6erawidyaqnht2uzdkhvk5vkslboyz8a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Years later, the Saints in Brazil were busy building a temple. The Martins family was excited, but they were also sad. They would not be able to enter the temple after it was dedicated. “Don’t worry,” Helvécio told Rudá. “The Lord knows everything.”

Saints, [4:293–94](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/18-all-the-blessings?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1)

---

![The Martins talking with a friend.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/nekvaj1dg91o6qn30cm1ah9hr1ckusrqfpp1m4vs/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

It wasn’t easy to remain faithful. People made fun of the Martins family. Even their friends couldn’t understand why they stayed in the Church. But Helvécio and his family knew that it was the Church of Jesus Christ.

Saints, [4:251–52](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/15-the-joy-of-an-eternal-covenant?lang=eng&id=p33-p40#p33)

---

![Rudá talking with Helvécio about the end of the temple and priesthood retrictions.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/qkodu7l2dfn9eoazkmizfr88v9knl3wgdclyo67e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, when Helvécio came home from work, Rudá said, “I have news, amazing news!” After much fasting and prayer, the prophet, President Spencer W. Kimball, had received a revelation. God told him that anyone, no matter their skin color, could have every blessing of the priesthood and the temple.

[Official Declaration 2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/od/2?lang=eng); Saints, [4:318–19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/19-united-as-a-family?lang=eng&id=p34-p36#p34)

---

![The Martins family worshipping at the temple.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bjglx275j0h2mph4gfh8suhhfzoqsrukub38nmqu/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Martins family and many others had waited so long. Now the wait was over! Helvécio and Marcus received the priesthood. Helvécio, Rudá, and their children were sealed as a family in the temple. They could now receive all the blessings of the gospel.

Saints, [4:319–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v4/part-2/19-united-as-a-family?lang=eng&id=p40-p48#p40)
