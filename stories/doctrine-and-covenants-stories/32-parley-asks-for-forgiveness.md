A friend’s testimony builds his faith

January–July 1837

---

![Thankful and Parley Pratt holding their newborn baby.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c0yieowwmld25cnz7q46xjb6toajkgy2eojokolg/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When it was time for Thankful and Parley Pratt to have their baby, they came home from their mission in Canada. They had a healthy baby boy just as the Lord had promised they would.

Saints, [1:270](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p45#p45)

---

![Parley holding the baby at Thankful’s grave.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fm2j803j4sms3fgxotszrukjyvk8ikraf9k4hltl/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Sadly, Thankful died a few hours after the baby was born. Parley knew he wouldn’t be able to take care of the baby without her. He asked a woman in Kirtland to care for the baby while he went back to Canada to finish his mission.

Saints, [1:270](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p45#p45)

---

![Joseph Smith talking to people about the new bank.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4ahvh2r5be774g4zdic9dt6xz6vojxb1aah87p9z/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In Kirtland, Joseph Smith started a bank to raise money for God’s work. He asked people to let the bank use their money so others could borrow it. But, for many reasons, the bank didn’t have enough money and had to close. Other banks in the United States were failing too.

Saints, [1:260–61](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p2-p5#p2), [264–68](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p18-p36#p18)

---

![Saints arguing about the bank inside the Kirtland Temple.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/mkp4usx551i7vkkbsa01wbo5bwpneu8hlznqoxqy/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Life became hard for the Saints. Some lost their jobs and now didn’t have enough money to buy food or clothes. Some people were angry at Joseph, even some of his close friends. They thought Joseph wasn’t a true prophet because the bank failed.

Saints, [1:264–68](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p18-p36#p18), [272](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p56-p59#p56) – [275](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p4#p4)

---

![Parley holding his infant child.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/xvluf41y6phn7qgmte6jw7nlep1jrpm61sy27yhe/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

While this was happening, Parley came home from his mission. The problems with the bank made life hard for Parley too. He wouldn’t have enough money to pay for his house, and he wouldn’t have a place to live.

Saints, [1:270–71](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p46-p50#p46)

---

![Parley writing a letter to Joseph.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/lcsskyow4pwuljetxxna7z01rxqr8cpr2778ilxy/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Now Parley was angry with Joseph too. He wrote Joseph a letter. He told Joseph that he still believed in the Book of Mormon. But he also said unkind things about the Prophet. He told Joseph he was wrong to start the bank in the first place.

Saints, [1:271](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/23-every-snare?lang=eng&id=p51-p53#p51)

---

![Parley greeting friends from Canada.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/qgqheww8sj45gltevu37dapaupzlq3scsh7b2pgl/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Later, some of Parley’s friends from Canada came to Kirtland. Parley had taught them the gospel, and they were happy to see him again.

Saints, [1:279–80](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p26-p27#p26)

---

![Parley’s friends observing while Parley and others argue with Joseph.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1erui7422htolcorh27j84vni3ei7n55cadxfrlr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But they were also surprised that Parley and so many others had lost their faith that Joseph Smith was a prophet.

Saints, [1:279–80](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p26-p27#p26)

---

![Parley talking with John Taylor.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8f5vlgv24ag773qd5hmtkqads86azf9v1gusooad/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One of Parley’s friends from Canada was John Taylor. When Parley saw him, he told John how angry he was at Joseph Smith. He told John not to follow Joseph anymore.

Saints, [1:280](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

---

![John sharing his testimony with Parley.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f2a7y3t6021amwpdmgm26yfouxqcy3rj06ufemdv/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John reminded Parley that when they were in Canada, Parley had shared his testimony that Joseph Smith was a true prophet. “I now have the same testimony,” John said. “If the work was true six months ago, it is true today. If Joseph Smith was then a prophet, he is now a prophet.”

Saints, [1:280](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p28-p29#p28)

---

![Parley observing Joseph and others inside the Kirtland Temple.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/lzp4n56lj5vw0qn8v0ywr1tvj9x6ypq77l1i7cpg/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Parley knew his friend John was right. He felt sorry about his angry letter to Joseph. He knew that Joseph was doing his best to do the Lord’s work. The problems with the bank did not mean that Joseph was not a true prophet of Jesus Christ.

Saints, [1:283](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p45-p46#p45)

---

![Parley apologizing to Joseph.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/mqadjm4biuas56uozocdvl6uig2q10l2nelnz3xq/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Parley went to Joseph’s home. He told Joseph that he was sorry about the unkind things he had said. Joseph forgave Parley, prayed for him, and blessed him. Parley served many more missions and told people all over the world that Joseph Smith was the Lord’s prophet.

[Doctrine and Covenants 64:7–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/64?lang=eng&id=p7-p11#p7); Saints, [1:283](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/saints-v1/24-truth-shall-prevail?lang=eng&id=p47#p47)
