Read about this story in [Doctrine and Covenants 109–110](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/109?lang=eng).

---

![Joseph Smith seeing image of Kirtland Temple](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/536983a5582d72e2df333b4514d8917ceb5c432e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

God told Joseph Smith to build a temple. It would be a house of God.

---

![children bringing water to workers building the temple](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/3dcc172943b0a24c2661f1a8c99ca0fb3e7ecc0b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Saints worked together to help build the temple. Men built tall walls. Women made curtains and carpets. Children helped bring tools and water to the workers.

---

![family singing together](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c31704464de10b1f80633b905c72a1bc6701c69e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the temple was finished, the Saints went inside. They sang a hymn and shouted “Hosanna!” Joseph said a prayer to dedicate (bless) the temple.

---

![Jesus appearing to Joseph and Oliver inside temple](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/481e8ca02a6ee8eb8489ece5d67565e21ce6dec6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A week later, Jesus Christ visited Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery in the temple. Jesus said that He accepted the temple as His house.

---

![three children holding hands and standing outside temple](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b372d5a6876f18f960b050f222be02236f3c2254/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Today there are temples all over the world! Each temple is a holy place. Someday I will go inside and make promises with God.
