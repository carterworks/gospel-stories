1:6

Read about this story in [Doctrine and Covenants 115–120](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/115?lang=eng).

---

![image of Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c0fdd2fb0b4011ecb305eeeeac1e1a1b92677bf4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus Christ told Joseph Smith what to name the Church. He said it would be called “The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.”

---

![Church sign outside chapel](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bb8304550b4011ec80e5eeeeac1e3718ccbb2c22/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The words *Jesus Christ* are the most important part of the Church’s name. Since it is Jesus Christ’s Church, it is named after Him.

---

![children holding scriptures in different languages](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bb90e6ca0b4011eca545eeeeac1e66597675338e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

*Latter-day Saints* means the members of the Church today.

---

![boy and grandma watching prophet during conference](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bb7072070b4011ecbf70eeeeac1e58e7d75357a9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Prophets and apostles have taught that using the correct name of the Church is important. It helps others know we believe in Jesus Christ.

---

![girl showing friend painting of Jesus at her house](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c05a079b0b4011eca545eeeeac1e6659e2beb633/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When others ask me what I believe, I can say, “I believe in Jesus Christ. I belong to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.”
