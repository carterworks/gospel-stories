![Joseph Smith teaching crowd](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0f4143d4d6d2a2ecddcad889a94a7bbfe4939e83/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph Smith was a prophet. He helped Jesus bring His Church back to the world.

---

![the first vision illustration](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c960e04ade1b0280be0f7c5e82e5ba55894085ba/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph prayed to find out which church he should go to. Jesus said not to go to any of the churches. None of them were His Church.

---

![Joseph Smith finds gold plates](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/02ed80e7565149301a1a5e6cdeb9d27b1b5d1559/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The angel Moroni visited Joseph. Moroni told him about a special book written on gold plates. It was buried in a hill nearby.

---

![Joseph Smith translates the gold plates](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f9be4d4e4d8a02ba509b247ef7e2d030e9927dc6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

With Heavenly Father’s help, Joseph translated the writing on the plates. These writings became the Book of Mormon!

---

![children talking at church](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7c4e11501815e752eee9975f8b1b153a74359776/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Now we can read the Book of Mormon. And we can go to Jesus Christ’s Church!
