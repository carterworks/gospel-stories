Read about this story in [Doctrine and Covenants 76](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng).

---

![Heavenly Father and Jesus appearing to Joseph and Sidney](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0a33953dc7db11eb978aeeeeac1e7c4f64516249/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day Joseph Smith and Sidney Rigdon were reading the scriptures. They had a vision. They saw Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

---

![Heavenly Father and Jesus in heaven](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0887b9f4c7db11eba14feeeeac1ea2a913367ac8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

They learned that before we were born, we lived in heaven with God.

---

![mom hugging son](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/095717e6c7db11eba14feeeeac1ea2a936d18a95/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

We came to earth to learn how to follow Jesus and listen to the Holy Ghost. We learn to keep the commandments and repent when we make bad choices.

---

![Jesus at the tomb](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/08f43768c7db11eb85a7eeeeac1edcf4901fde71/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After we die, we will be resurrected. If we are righteous, we will live with our Heavenly Father again.

---

![father reading scripture to children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0954f50ac7db11eb8fbbeeeeac1ef1c8fc0284ea/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can learn about God’s plan in the scriptures. Because of Jesus, I can be with my family forever.
