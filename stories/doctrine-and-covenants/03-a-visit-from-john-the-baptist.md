---

![Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery pray](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/13d56133418d6745d2d529ccff22cfe0d4881c57/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery had a question. What was the right way to be baptized? They prayed to know the answer.

---

![John the Baptist](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a3d0e82101d36c61b517a463969b41a365a5b10b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

An angel came. It was John the Baptist. He had baptized Jesus. He told them that they needed the priesthood to baptize people.

---

![John the Baptist blesses Joseph Smith](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ab25f5e0f4b0bf5da4648401b9f1241f025e7d36/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John blessed Joseph and Oliver. He gave them the priesthood. Now they could baptize people the way Jesus was baptized.

---

![Oliver Cowdery baptizes Joseph Smith](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fa6ce657797b853be18afb37e957e5cdd1208bf0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph baptized Oliver in the river. Then Oliver baptized Joseph. Later, many others were baptized too.

---

![man baptizes child](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/862e460ea38bdee6d26bed2eb7ef5a556adf82f2/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Because Heavenly Father answered Joseph Smith’s prayer, the priesthood is back on earth! I can be baptized like Jesus was.
