---

![a pioneer boy and girl walking through a field of flowers next to covered wagons](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bd051af949e2b9170f3df670c905753faee1a207/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord told the members of the Church to move far away. Some drove covered wagons. Some pulled handcarts

---

![pioneer children helping cleanup at building site for Salt Lake Temple](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8675676066835416884c86b59be73639a3492780/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

They went to a new place to live. They built houses and towns. They built new temples.

---

![a woman being baptized](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b7f7df917cf9246f2b9a2343f477d4f1cc47e427/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Missionaries went to many countries. They taught others about Jesus Christ. More people were baptized. The Church grew.

---

![angel Moroni statue in front of map of world](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/58513a6927b611ec9b75eeeeac1e708da35c70e4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Today, Church members live all over the world. There are temples in many countries.

---

![two children standing below clothesline](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/56cb0fce27b611ec86faeeeeac1e09d21b670e11/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

No matter where we live, Jesus loves us. I know He loves me!
