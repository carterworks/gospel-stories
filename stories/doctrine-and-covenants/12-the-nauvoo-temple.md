Read about this story in [Doctrine and Covenants 125–128](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/125?lang=eng).

---

![pioneer family in Nauvoo](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8cfbb5240ba611ecb305eeeeac1e1a1bf0d101ee/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Saints built a new city called Nauvoo. God told them to build a temple there.

---

![a pioneer man carving a large stone](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8d87b6ee0ba611ecb3a9eeeeac1e6802f4d67000/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Saints worked hard to build it. They built it near a big river. They carved suns and stars into the stone.

---

![a man filling the baptism font with water](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8d8fcced0ba611ec9ee7eeeeac1e7c5387815139/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord said to build a baptism font inside the temple. It was filled with water.

---

![pioneer family in front of a gravestone](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8d72339b0ba611ec80e5eeeeac1e37185289bff3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

People were baptized for family members who died before they could be baptized.

---

![a family standing together in front of the temple](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8e82440d0ba611ec80e5eeeeac1e37189c452335/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Because of temples, I can be with my family forever. Someday I can go inside to do temple baptisms.
