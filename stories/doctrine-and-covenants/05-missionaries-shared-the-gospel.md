After the Book of Mormon was printed, God called missionaries to go and teach more people about it.

---

![missionary speaking to woman and child](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/60391974635c9ddd13d89f9ead4496d0e4d1a361/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph Smith’s brother Samuel carried a bag full of copies of the Book of Mormon to share. People gathered to hear him teach.

---

![missionaries meeting with family on beach](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4f2fe17712e9681c3a00733baa557c100010340a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Other missionaries taught people in Europe. Some taught people who lived on islands.

---

![sister missionaries speaking to woman in city](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2fb427aaeaf9c75f0c1f566affa63d35787f4b3b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Later, the first official sister missionaries were called to serve in England.

---

![children reading scriptures together](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/246c0ec9ee05177f915455af79677a9d5b3d840b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can be a missionary too. I can share the Book of Mormon with others.
