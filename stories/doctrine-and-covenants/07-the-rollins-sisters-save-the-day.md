1:16

Read about this story in [Doctrine and Covenants 67–70](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/67?lang=eng).

---

![two men printing scripture pages](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8d9f6926f0fed0901bfe28caa647b13aed695b86/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus Christ told the Prophet Joseph Smith many important things to help us. The Church set up a printing press to print Jesus’s words. That way people would be able to read them.

---

![scripture pages being thrown on street](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/53e9c3a413cefa926827c898b325c85b183f2ecc/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, a group of angry men came. They destroyed the printing press. They threw the pages of the scriptures out into the street.

---

![two girls holding scripture pages](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c3b4281e852bcae5d58a7607f9c648febfa17832/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Two sisters named Mary Elizabeth and Caroline Rollins saw what happened. They ran and gathered the pages in their arms.

---

![girls hiding in cornfield](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f0ce8b6be37eddee591fda251eaf67d00c9feb47/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The girls hid in a cornfield until the men left. They had saved the scriptures! Those scriptures later became part of the Doctrine and Covenants.

---

![child and grandma reading scriptures on bus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/83fc47ef57a74b1a40e90661db9bca4290717cb5/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can read the scriptures. I can be brave and stand up for what I believe.
