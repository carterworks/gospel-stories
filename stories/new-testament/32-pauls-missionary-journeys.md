1:14

[Philippians 4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/philip/4?lang=eng)

The Apostle Paul traveled to different lands to teach people about Jesus. There were no cars or airplanes back then, so he walked a lot! Sometimes he traveled by ship.

---

![Paul teaching people on a city street](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/df69e13c535f99794ffcdd10f8a3c013fdee9275/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul taught people in churches and homes. He taught people on rocky hilltops and city streets.

---

![Paul looking at a bird from behind prison bars](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/71e3f5f9257fe828850e12bd3ca5c95f5c6e7c3b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Many people didn’t like what Paul taught. Sometimes Paul got sent to prison. Sometimes Paul got sick.

---

![Paul walking down a pathway](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f3c0dfc690b4673a5e8be673ca95e394adb7153d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But through all these hard things, Paul had faith. He said, “I can do all things through Christ.” He knew Jesus would help him be strong.

---

![parents helping a girl ride her bike](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/12979a9e2536052c0cd4041a47738a8217ffbad4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus Christ can help me be strong too. He loves me, and I love Him!
