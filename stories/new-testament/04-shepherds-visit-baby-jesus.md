One night, an angel visited shepherds in a field. The angel told them that an important baby was born. His name was Jesus Christ. He would be the Savior for all people in the world. More angels came. They sang, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men.”

The shepherds hurried to visit baby Jesus. They were so happy to see Him!
