One night Jesus Christ and His disciples crossed the sea in a boat. Jesus was tired and fell asleep.

---

![A storm tipping the boat with Jesus and His disciples](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/90d86a251e3711ed892aeeeeac1ea2c39dcc499c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A storm came. The waves got bigger and bigger! The disciples were afraid. They woke Jesus up and asked Him to save them.

---

![Jesus calming the storm](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9358fa7f1e3711edae6beeeeac1e8c2bf34b777d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus stood up and said, “Peace, be still.” The wind stopped. The water was calm.

---

![Little boy folding his arms with his eyes closed](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/970e61621e3711edb5f5eeeeac1ef4aea14f736d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

My faith in Jesus Christ can bring me peace. When I’m afraid, Jesus can help me feel calm.
