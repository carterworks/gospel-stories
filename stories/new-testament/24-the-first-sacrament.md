Before He died, Jesus Christ met with His Apostles. He gave them the sacrament.

---

![Jesus holding bread](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b56a3c3465ce11ed92cfeeeeac1e5d92e597a712/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus broke bread and gave it to them. He asked them to eat it to help them remember that He gave His life for them.

---

![Jesus holding cup](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b8a3201765ce11eda13aeeeeac1e6dc4bec8495c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Jesus gave them a cup. He asked them to drink from it. It would help them remember Him too.

---

![Early Christians taking the sacrament](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ba6430ff65ce11ed9915eeeeac1e06460085f985/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Even after Jesus was not with His Apostles, the sacrament helped them think of Him. They could feel His love and remember to keep the commandments.
