One day Jesus Christ met 10 men. They were very sick.

---

![Men smiling after being healed](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/264761dd4e3c11ed8019eeeeac1e6a2f7404c7e1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

On their way, the men were healed! They knew Jesus had healed them.

---

![Man kneeling in front of Jesus Christ](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/296f55d14e3c11ed84a1eeeeac1e55abd8c011b2/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One of the 10 men came back to Jesus. He knelt at Jesus’s feet and thanked Him. Jesus said he was healed because he had faith.
