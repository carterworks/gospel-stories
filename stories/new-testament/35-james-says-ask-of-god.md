James was an Apostle. He taught others about Jesus Christ. He wrote a letter to the people who believed in Jesus.

---

![alt text](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/3aa65903df8811ed8f90eeeeac1e4427bc560360/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In his letter, James taught people to pray. He told them to have faith. He said they could ask God to know what is true.

---

![alt text](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/3f40efc3df8811eda194eeeeac1e1924cfa01841/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Hundreds of years later, Joseph Smith read James’s letter in the Bible.

---

![alt text](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4325a404df8811eda194eeeeac1e192440ecb3af/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

He asked God what church to join.

Joseph’s prayer was answered! Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ appeared to Joseph. They helped him bring back Jesus Christ’s Church on the earth.
