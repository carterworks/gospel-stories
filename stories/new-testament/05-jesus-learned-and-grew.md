Jesus was born as a little baby. I was a baby once too!

---

![Mary and Joseph feeding young Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5e5df42196a5c26b04bff07c7e5806eb75b69ce0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Mary and Joseph helped take care of Jesus. Who helps take care of me?

---

![Joseph teaching young Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0d2cb79743ed6d3691699c676ca1b68c60df50f0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus grew in the same ways I am growing. He grew in *wisdom.* That means He learned new things.

---

![young Jesus standing](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/29433d3745555c81041833f53302ea1e59d4421b/full/%21500%2C/0/default)

He grew in *stature.* That means His body got bigger.

---

![young Jesus reading](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7d05316592d665bd3e0a5b9eccd4f938fca45dc6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

He grew in favor *with God.* That means He learned about Heavenly Father. He learned to pray. He read the scriptures.

---

![young Jesus playing with friends](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/aa4751fe59dbb349c081b6a3fa96b378dfe75ced/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

He grew in favor *with man.* That means He made new friends. He was kind to others. He helped his family.

---

![children gardening](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/d00086dfa394b8d14ea54187fc8c01c591264a50/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can help my mind and body grow. I can learn about God. I can be a good friend. I will grow in good ways, like Jesus did!
