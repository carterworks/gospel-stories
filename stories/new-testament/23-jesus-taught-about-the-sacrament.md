![the Last Supper](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/29e87752002bb32b4f2ca1d2d2a6c78c9482fade/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Before Jesus died, He ate a special meal with His disciples. It was called the Last Supper.

---

![Jesus breaking bread](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/df4b79e71b799d72023f34fb2a1d7e2f0f4d67fd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus took bread and blessed it. He gave it to His disciples. He asked them to eat it to help them remember Him.

---

![Jesus holding cup](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/854590dc6c6dc1f6cc4bb606b78804bd9ae0536e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus took a cup and gave it to His disciples. He told them to drink from it to help them remember Him.

---

![Jesus and His disciples singing](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7eb12c00be7bbc2e091bc3dd311388319de84a77/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus and His disciples also sang a hymn together.
