![Paul yelling at man](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/844d89cdb2342a49e34d0611a7cf6286cfde6bfd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After Jesus died, His disciples kept teaching people. But not everyone liked what they taught. A man named Paul was especially mean to those who followed Jesus.

---

![light shines on Paul](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/cdee3b57ff64194b3ea3faba2dd1190feb8ca9cd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day while Paul was traveling, he saw a bright light from heaven. He heard Jesus’s voice! Jesus told Paul to repent and follow Him.

---

![Paul covering his eyes](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/971bf48b53e35c6cb71f400b836c1d520290aa8d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The bright light made Paul blind. A holy man named Ananias healed Paul’s eyes. Paul chose to be baptized.

---

![Paul preaches to crowd](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/861f78621b4bf040b787d6ec216a90134840ab25/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After that, Paul taught people about Jesus Christ. He was a great missionary, teacher, and leader in Christ’s Church.

---

![girl comforting friend](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/17215aa1e82fb7c46702b7967de3bf11ac56bbd4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can choose to be kind instead of being mean. I can always choose to follow Jesus.
