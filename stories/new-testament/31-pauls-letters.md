[1 Corinthians 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/1?lang=eng) and [Galatians 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/gal/1?lang=eng)

The Apostle Paul helped lead the Church after Jesus returned to heaven. Back then, there were no telephones or internet. So Paul wrote letters to members of the Church.

---

![woman reading letter](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f56f2e4b1194ef976eb2d145a2762e8d3c6247eb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul wrote to people in the city of Corinth. He told them to forgive others. He told them to be patient. He wrote about Jesus.

---

![man reading letter](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/566c62d2afab13e4c1508786731ec98a58bd7c6d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul wrote to people in a place called Galatia. He told them to love their neighbors. He told them what the Spirit feels like.

---

![child reading book](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b126bd2f675b39882088662c83d1f5c83846de45/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul’s letters were so important that they became part of the Bible. You can read them in the New Testament.
