One day Jesus was teaching people. Some families came to Him with their babies and little children. They wanted Jesus to bless their children.

---

![disciples talking to people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f866cd0ce1cad61daa819ea2b7ce5086014fa1c3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The disciples didn’t want the children to bother Jesus. They tried to send them away.

---

![Jesus talking to disciples](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0c5069b6f81549f0adb3bc4f1bc05bcea4026c3a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus told His disciples to let the little children come to Him. He said that grown-ups need to have faith like little children to get to heaven.

---

![Jesus blessing children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b1f0a6adff71482e02434664da4584c493dc5e77/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Jesus took the children in His arms and blessed them.

---

![mother and two children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f999843d8b2854c30e3d9fbef28bf7fee481e6d9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus loves children. He wants me to be kind to people everywhere.
