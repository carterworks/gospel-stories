At Christmastime, we celebrate the birth of Jesus Christ.

---

![Mary and Joseph hugging baby Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/3b06fe653c0553c36cc5140adfffd03e1114c015/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus was born in Bethlehem. His mother was named Mary, and Joseph was her husband.

---

![Simeon holding baby Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fe0faa39e6dd6d628cc3faf8638fedeb7c2c827d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Mary and Joseph took baby Jesus to the temple in Jerusalem. There, a man named Simeon held baby Jesus. He felt the Holy Ghost tell him that Jesus was the Son of God.

---

![Anna proclaiming to crowd](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b147fac1329bf053b3fc717c0d83a1a13f77127b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A woman named Anna also saw baby Jesus and knew He was the Savior. She went to tell other people about Him.

---

![child holding lantern](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9d1d5ef05336a9567f67b216bff9ccda27337299/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I think about Jesus at Christmastime. He really lives. He loves me!
