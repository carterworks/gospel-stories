Mary and Martha had a brother named Lazarus. They were Jesus Christ’s friends.

---

![Jesus hugging Mary and Martha](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/251c2545338211edad31eeeeac1e4a1bd01a5aee/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Lazarus became very sick. Soon, he died. Mary and Martha were sad. When Jesus came, He cried with them.

---

![Jesus standing in Lazarus’s tomb](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2568bea9338211edb3f2eeeeac1e2b2b0510900a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Jesus went with them to Lazarus’s tomb. He said, “Lazarus, come forth.”

---

![Jesus helping Lazarus come out of the tomb](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/25a419f8338211ed9569eeeeac1e4416f23dafda/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Lazarus stood and walked out of the tomb. He was alive again! Because of Jesus, we will all live again too!
