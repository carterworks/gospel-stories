![angel appearing to Mary](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/134cd0188c6afbd8da9aad2fad1b699ef9e8dd80/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

An angel came to Mary and told her that God was happy with her. She was going to be Jesus’s mother!

---

![Joseph and Mary at an inn](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/31b899aeefa6b0a027479ede92122fb55cbe8154/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Mary and Joseph traveled to Bethlehem. It was almost time for Jesus to be born. So many people were visiting Bethlehem that Mary and Joseph couldn’t find a room to stay in. An innkeeper let them stay in a stable.

---

![star above a stable](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0cf071f53343da912268f51287363a986f50ba72/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Soon Jesus was born. A bright new star appeared to tell everyone that the Light of the World had come to earth.

---

![angel appearing to shepherds](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b0a2d68a66ac476db0eb8dd639d16e018b2da4dc/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

An angel told some shepherds that Jesus was born. They hurried to see the baby Jesus lying in a manger.

---

![family decorating for Christmas](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ddd88ceeae07c1793646c1b91642d494cb5c9143/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus came to earth because He loves me. I will follow His light this Christmas and all year!
