![John the Baptist teaching people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/83aaad997b85026158e0f480286438d8471c9a6a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John the Baptist was a great prophet. He taught people to repent and baptized them.

---

![John the Baptist baptizing people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0cd17d9c8a99b5cba8edabfc6bb0890f840ee950/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day John was baptizing people in the Jordan River. Jesus came and asked John to baptize Him. John knew that Jesus didn’t have any sins. So why did Jesus want to be baptized?

---

![Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5a32eeed2e4175c5c0fdcbc127a294a95f9a37aa/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus said He needed to obey all of the commandments. Being baptized is a commandment.

---

![John the Baptist baptizing Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/28481f65bf8f3831a7375da1f07777b95f25dbba/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After John baptized Jesus, a dove came to show that the Holy Ghost was there. Heavenly Father’s voice came from heaven, saying, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” ([Matthew 3:17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng&id=p17#p17)).

---

![father and daughter in baptism clothes](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4c54b8f4b633c4a768c2668402a7fa83501bce7d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

We can obey the commandments and choose to be baptized, like Jesus. Then we can be confirmed and have the gift of the Holy Ghost too.
