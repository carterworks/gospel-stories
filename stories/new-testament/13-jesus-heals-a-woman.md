One day Jesus walked down a busy street. In the crowd was a woman who had been sick for 12 years.

---

![5 panels depicting the story of Jesus Healing the Women with an issue of blood. There is also an illustration depicting a mondern day application.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6b4729f306d511ed92bbeeeeac1e6bee49e20121/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The woman had faith that Jesus could heal her. She reached out and touched His robe. Right away she was healed!

---

![5 panels depicting the story of Jesus Healing the Women with an issue of blood. There is also an illustration depicting a mondern day application.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6b8df6f506d511ed97f8eeeeac1e98d3f18a6559/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

“Who touched my clothes?” Jesus asked. The woman was afraid. She knelt in front of Jesus and told Him what happened.

---

![5 panels depicting the story of Jesus Healing the Women with an issue of blood. There is also an illustration depicting a mondern day application.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6bcdbf1406d511ed92bbeeeeac1e6bee3b95a00b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus was loving. He said, “Be of good comfort.” He told her she was healed because of her faith.

---

![5 panels depicting the story of Jesus Healing the Women with an issue of blood. There is also an illustration depicting a mondern day application.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6c0e98a406d511edbc5feeeeac1e2bf04f2a73c6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can have faith in Jesus Christ. His love can help me and bring me peace.
