Have you ever seen someone get baptized? When we get baptized, we’re following Jesus’s example.

---

![Jesus talking to John](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/cd8c13936b528fea1f368f92cf259fd279b8ccc3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus asked His cousin John to baptize Him. John had the priesthood authority to baptize people.

---

![John talking to Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7fb5d04b7c6c3f1e4a9e2675fdcc29d12b716898/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John asked Jesus why He wanted to get baptized. Jesus said He wanted to obey Heavenly Father’s commandments.

---

![John baptizing Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a28a91990c3f3f0ae13d3b8d9073b0cfa4c649b1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John baptized Jesus. The Holy Ghost came. John and Jesus heard Heavenly Father’s voice say, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”

---

![child helping woman carry groceries](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/baf3e9ad176e57fae2bdb35bf65d73f662fe5018/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day I can choose to be baptized, like Jesus was. I can get ready for baptism by trying to be like Jesus every day.
