When Jesus Christ was born, a new star rose in the sky. Wise Men knew the star meant that Jesus had been born. They followed the star. They traveled many days.

---

![Three wise men ride on camels as they head toward Bethlehem. The star of Bethlehem goes before them.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e1d766ebf24811ecb0e0eeeeac1e7f2de1420109/full/%21640%2C/0/default) ![A young Jesus Christ is presented with gifts by three Wise men.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e02f352bf24811ecac1feeeeac1ed8072716ce00/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the Wise Men found Jesus, they gave Him gifts. They knelt before Him and worshipped Him. They knew He was the Son of God.

---

![A brother and sister help shovel snow of a sidewalk and stairs in front of their grandparent's home.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e3978d72f24811ec9d20eeeeac1eb431f103e0bf/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can give gifts to Jesus too. I can show my love for Him when I follow His example and show love to others.
