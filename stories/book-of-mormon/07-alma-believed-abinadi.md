![Abinadi preaching to King Noah](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/32f8981b79a8e0a1150fadba8b88e28d3e27f9ae/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In the Book of Mormon, we learn about a prophet named Abinadi. God sent him to teach the wicked people of King Noah.

Abinadi told the king and his followers to repent and keep God’s commandments. They didn’t listen. They were angry with the prophet! They didn’t want to change.

---

![Abinadi teach Alma](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6d62f7d18bbde0ffe5143efcb3977c02b302db2b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But one person believed the prophet. His name was Alma. He decided to repent and leave the wicked king.

---

![Alma creating gold plates](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5352100af7c7f0bc580427e1dc8de6021994f28e/full/%21640%2C/0/default) ![Alma teaching people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c86e79f03f703fb6ad843870effaaedb6ab3c187/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Alma wrote down what Abinadi had taught. He started teaching other people about the gospel.

---

![people watching and reading general conference](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4483ff8b8ea885344ac917b0759fdc9b19c20186/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I will listen to the prophet. Even when other people make bad choices, I can always choose to follow God.
