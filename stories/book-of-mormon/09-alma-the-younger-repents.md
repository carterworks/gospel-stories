![Alma talking with people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f6ce6261a34db8ed5c1eabb0c7a7b9f3b3de7cf7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Alma was the prophet’s son. He was named for his father, but he didn’t believe what his father taught. He told people that the Church wasn’t true.

---

![angel appearing to Alma and others](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1e7a1c0ab5bd169bf218111e644cd312e75ccde6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, an angel appeared to Alma. The angel told Alma to repent.

---

![Alma looking asleep](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/58c221166bc89b871a1d0c424ff0e0927ce650bd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

For three days, it was like Alma was asleep. He was thinking about his bad choices.

---

![Alma teaching others](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a758557fc794ad4b21269518efd9fd68a7f040ad/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Alma prayed to be forgiven. And he was! He felt Jesus Christ’s love. Alma woke up and taught others about how Jesus saved him.

---

![kids in a car](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ab9219ff3a582d79814ce42a3b4cc435ca136429/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can repent when I make a wrong choice. Jesus will always be there for me.
