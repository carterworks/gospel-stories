---

![Jesus talking to children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/44a5fcf64290b8803029db2d5104a8d6da1f42c3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Jesus lived on the earth, He healed people who were sick or hurt. He taught people how to pray. He blessed the children.

---

![Jesus and Apostles taking the sacrament](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4ea51f053aff202d82fe98e971039db64205f1d6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

He called 12 Apostles and gave them the sacrament to help them remember Him. Then Jesus died and was resurrected.

---

![Jesus visiting Nephites](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6e082821831f7da39af7dbd1c5c2ca48d9d38a01/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After He was resurrected, Jesus went to visit the Nephites, who lived across the ocean.

---

![Jesus administering priesthood](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a31a7cd91ca34abc509c59114386a5ad01e783c1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

He called 12 disciples and gave them the priesthood. He healed people who were sick or hurt. He taught people how to pray. Everyone bowed down and worshipped Him.

---

![Jesus blessing children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e38cf1b350ee1968b62c6ce1479902caa665a66a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus told the Nephites to bring their children to Him. He prayed, and then He blessed each child. Angels came to visit them.

---

![Jesus giving Nephites the sacrament](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/24c8c2232a472d1903ee867c9237e4a2dab2daac/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus gave the Nephites the sacrament so they would always remember Him.
