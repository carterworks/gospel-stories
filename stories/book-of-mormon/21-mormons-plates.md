Read about this in [Mormon 1–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/1?lang=eng).

---

![Mormon showing children how to write on the plates](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7654d3ebc5e1baf9aa0c1d4dc9cf323a8fa2fa07/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In Book of Mormon times, people didn’t have books or computers like we do now. So they wrote important things on thin pieces of metal called plates.

---

![Jesus visiting with children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0952225c633bdab55eb676595419b4282464c8d8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Prophets like Nephi, Alma, and Ether wrote on lots of metal plates. They wrote what happened to their people. They wrote about the gospel of Jesus Christ.

---

![Mormon holding the plates](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/261e61072848e8044ef991cdea7ec2ee23c9aefb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

God asked a prophet named Mormon to gather the most important parts from all of these plates. Mormon and his son, Moroni, wrote them all on a set of gold plates.

---

![the Book of Mormon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/89fddc4516aaf95fa9f63325d9e62172a06e2d1e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Because Mormon was the one who put most of this book together, it is called the Book of Mormon. Like the Bible, it teaches about Jesus Christ.

---

![two girls reading the Book of Mormon together](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/80f1fa802b4e75416e831538a1743efcea73e0f9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can learn about Jesus by reading the Book of Mormon. Even though these prophets lived long ago, their words help me today.
