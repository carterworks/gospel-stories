A woman named Abish worked for the king. She believed in God. Most people around her did not believe in God. One day she saw a missionary named Ammon teaching the king and queen about God and Jesus Christ.

---

![king and queen sleeping](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/51af7ba9c5e368b8fe3fe7d33c34902246376ed5/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the king and queen learned about the gospel, they were so happy! They fell down and didn’t move. Abish knew that they were feeling the power of God.

---

![Abish running](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/efbb5cfca84f683bdbf43245a6052cbe060ecfd8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abish wanted everyone to learn about God. So she ran from house to house and told people to come and see what had happened.

---

![queen helping king to stand](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8d8bc48bd4b3cb0b6377d2e2a7b7381b6de8bc2e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After the crowd gathered, Abish helped the queen stand up. Then the queen helped the king stand up. They started teaching people about the gospel.

---

![children with umbrellas](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/06aac6c97fa2cf09a623d589d5d24baff7b55ece/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can be like Abish. I can help other people learn about Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.
