---

![sons of Mosiah teaching people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/17c07c483e6ae31c02011842d95152aba1183e17/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ammon and his brothers were missionaries. Sometimes they were hungry and tired, but they kept teaching about Jesus.

---

![sons of Mosiah leaving on a mission](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c832a0fac80a22065c0f39bacf77833978043af4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ammon and his brothers went to a land far away to teach their enemies about Jesus.

---

![Ammon talking to King Lamoni](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ba9aaf3f8a36bcb1dde886b8eca3c7779b844258/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Some men captured Ammon and took him to King Lamoni. Ammon told the king he wanted to serve him.

---

![Ammon protecting sheep](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/026ab973b2a1b9779cdb7886cdb6f81b7fcd7649/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ammon worked hard. He protected the king’s sheep from robbers. The king was grateful. He listened to Ammon.

---

![Ammon shaking hands with King Lamoni](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/eaa619afd81edc3e0892d697758728523b017faa/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ammon taught the king about God and the scriptures. The king believed him. Many people were baptized because of Ammon’s good example.

We can help others by being a good example like Ammon!
