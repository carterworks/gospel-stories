One day in the wilderness, Nephi’s hunting bow broke. His family was worried. They didn’t know how they would get food without it.

---

![Nephi and Lehi and the Liahona](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a3db18b947c9f733d9d54d7a43769c23c43ee5ab/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Nephi had faith. He made a new bow and arrow. His father, Lehi, prayed for help.

They looked at the Liahona. It was a special compass the Lord gave them. When they kept the commandments, the Liahona showed them where to go.

![Nephi holding a bow](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/52651f88052d9a6049ead108950a3ffc50035255/full/%21640%2C/0/default)
