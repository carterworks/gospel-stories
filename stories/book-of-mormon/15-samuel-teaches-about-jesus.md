![Samuel climbing a wall](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fefa0afc5d820cec4b8108eb329544e853e19753/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Samuel was a prophet. He climbed onto a city wall to preach. He told the people they needed to repent and have faith in Jesus Christ.

---

![Samuel standing on a wall](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/661a30c8c68ee3ef6d00ef1bb147f3ff96c7fd76/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Samuel taught that Jesus would soon be born. A new star would shine. The sky would stay bright the whole night!

He taught that when Jesus died, it would be dark for three days. Many cities would be destroyed.

---

![arrows flying toward Samuel](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ca31968b59ca3fc240bb06a0ea4cf68220815c54/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Some people believed and were baptized. Others did not believe and were angry. They threw rocks and shot arrows at Samuel. But God protected him, and he wasn’t hurt.

---

![Samuel running from arrows](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/23a553139b4749098390e1b652d8ab075d683043/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The people who did not believe tried to capture Samuel. But Samuel got away.

---

![people looking at new star](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0c49d524746f8b6bb22b00ace0f62092f2e2295c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Five years later, Jesus was born. A new star shone in the sky. It was just like Samuel had taught!
