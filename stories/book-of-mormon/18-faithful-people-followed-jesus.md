Read about this story in [3 Nephi 17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/17?lang=eng) through [4 Nephi 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/4-ne/1?lang=eng).

---

![Jesus Christ breaking bread](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/3d3516c55f3f4cb62aeabff3bfff77137c607303/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Jesus visited the Nephites, He taught them about baptism and how to take the sacrament. He set up His Church.

---

![Jesus blessing children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e84180aa99302c8af4bf0873102ff191dd0c2f5b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus blessed each child. Angels came! Jesus healed the people and prayed for them. Then He went back to heaven.

---

![woman sharing food](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4a29d31905f0e415147dcb666c0ca0d242fff45e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After Jesus left, people kept doing what He taught. Everyone worked together and shared what they had, so no one was poor. Church leaders blessed those who were sick.

---

![people talking around a fire](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/06eed77d518e3e6ddfadda92bdcb4e55f738e3f8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Instead of fighting, people chose to get along. Everyone followed God’s commandments. And they were happy for a long time!

---

![children blowing bubbles](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/87e406016461fe6430aff5b1fa6c4d395c891c9d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

My life will be happier when I follow God’s commandments. I can share what I have with others. I can help people get along.
