Protecting his people

[Alma 51–52](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng)

---

![Teancum speaks to Captain Moroni at camp](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/14b55a7a825311eeb684eeeeac1ee293e8c13c5d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Teancum was a leader in Captain Moroni’s army. He was trying to keep the Nephite cities safe from the Lamanites.

[Alma 50:35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/50?lang=eng&id=p35#p35); [52:19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/52?lang=eng&id=p19#p19)

---

![Amalickiah and Lamanite soldiers with weapons](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1603ac66825311ee8d3feeeeac1e686683f4afb8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Amalickiah was a Nephite who became the Lamanite king. He wanted to rule over the Nephites too. He attacked the Nephites and took over many cities.

[Alma 47:1, 35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/47?lang=eng&id=p1,p35#p1); [48:1–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/48?lang=eng&id=p1-p4#p1); [51:23–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng&id=p23-p28#p23)

---

![Nephite army marching](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/167b4d73825311ee9eebeeeeac1e7c0e6ab1b23b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Teancum’s army went to stop Amalickiah’s army from attacking Nephite cities.

[Alma 51:28–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng&id=p28-p30#p28)

---

![armies face each other at sunset](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/154601bb825311eeb054eeeeac1e57643863bc3e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The armies fought for the whole day. Teancum and his army fought with more strength and skill than Amalickiah’s army. But neither army won. When it became dark, both armies stopped fighting so that they could rest.

[Alma 51:31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng&id=p31#p31) —32

---

![Teancum sits in front of campfire](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/156d86f2825311ee959eeeeeac1e329d69fa4883/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But Teancum did not rest. He and his servant secretly went to Amalickiah’s camp.

[Alma 51:33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng&id=p33#p33)

---

![Teancum stands under moonlight at Amalickiah’s tent](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/15e9e2d8825311eeb21feeeeac1e01e13cbe2dac/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Teancum sneaked into Amalickiah’s tent. He killed Amalickiah before Amalickiah could wake up. Then Teancum went back to his camp and told his soldiers to be ready to fight.

[Alma 51:33–36](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/51?lang=eng&id=p33-p36#p33)

---

![Lamanites wake up and look scared](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/14e19a98825311eeb581eeeeac1e675d25e98c16/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the Lamanites woke up, they found that Amalickiah was dead. They also saw that Teancum and his army were ready to fight them.

[Alma 52:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/52?lang=eng&id=p1#p1)

---

![Lamanites run away from Teancum’s army](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1502de3b825311eebd33eeeeac1e7ca628e425fa/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lamanites were afraid and ran away. Teancum’s plan had made the Lamanites too scared to attack more Nephite cities. Teancum now had time to make the Nephite cities safer. He worked hard to protect his people. He was able to keep many Nephite cities safe.

[Alma 52:2–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/52?lang=eng&id=p2-p10#p2)
