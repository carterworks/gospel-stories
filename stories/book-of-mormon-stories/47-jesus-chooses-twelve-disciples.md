Power to teach and baptize

[3 Nephi 11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng); [13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/13?lang=eng); [18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/18?lang=eng)

---

![Jesus Christ places His hands on a disciple’s head and blesses him, and people stand around them and pray](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/575b60d2074c11ef996ceeeeac1e66fabe7120f6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Jesus Christ came to the city Bountiful, He chose twelve people and gave them power to teach and baptize. He also gave them power to give the gift of the Holy Ghost. He called them His disciples. The disciples did the things that Jesus taught them to do.

[3 Nephi 11:1, 18–22, 41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p1,p18-p22,p41#p1); [12:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/12?lang=eng&id=p1#p1); [15:11–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/15?lang=eng&id=p11-p12#p11); [18:36–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/18?lang=eng&id=p36-p37#p36)

---

![Jesus Christ teaches, a disciple baptizes someone, and the other disciples listen and watch](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/56695fb2074c11ef8689eeeeac1e2ea4a875dd0f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus taught His disciples how to baptize. He told them the words they should say. He taught them to put the person under the water and help them come up again. Jesus said to always baptize the way He taught them to. He also taught them about the Holy Ghost.

[3 Nephi 11:22–28, 35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p22-p28,p35#p22)

---

![Jesus Christ talks to one of His disciples](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/555382e2074c11efaf97eeeeac1ea8e4cd9471db/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus wanted His disciples to get along with each other and not argue. He said that when they argued, they were not following His teachings.

[3 Nephi 11:28–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p28-p30#p28)

---

![Jesus Christ holds his hand out to the people, and the people smile and listen](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/54c63701074c11efb7c6eeeeac1e46fbd1b52216/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus taught the people to believe in Him, repent, and be baptized so that they could live with Heavenly Father again.

[3 Nephi 11:31–33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p31-p33#p31)

---

![Jesus Christ walks with His disciples and other people and teaches them](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5448c9a2074c11efa176eeeeac1e3094bdbca1e2/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus told His disciples that if they believe in Him, they believe in Heavenly Father too. The Holy Ghost would help them know that Jesus and Heavenly Father are real.

[3 Nephi 11:35–36](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p35-p36#p35)

---

![Jesus Christ sits with His disciples and the people and teaches them](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/53110cf1074c11efba43eeeeac1e24f02a39a845/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Jesus finished teaching His disciples, He told them to go and teach all the people. He said not to worry about what they would eat, drink, or wear. He told them that if they served Heavenly Father, Heavenly Father would take care of them.

[3 Nephi 11:41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng&id=p41#p41); [13:25–34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/13?lang=eng&id=p25-p34#p25)
