A mighty prayer to the Lord

[Enos 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng)

---

![Enos hunting](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4522c88cae4411eda553eeeeac1edea175e47f15/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jacob gave the plates to his son Enos. Enos wrote on them like Jacob did. One day, Enos went hunting in the forest. He remembered what his father taught about the Lord. Jacob had taught Enos that following the Lord would bring him joy.

[Jacob 7:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/7?lang=eng&id=p27#p27); [Enos 1:2–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng&id=p2-p3#p2)

---

![Enos praying during the day](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/457184cbae4411edb33aeeeeac1ef0d1e9c9cd29/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Enos wanted to feel the joy his father spoke about. He decided to pray to the Lord. He prayed all day and into the night.

[Enos 1:3–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng&id=p3-p4#p3)

---

![Enos looking up](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/45c8f39fae4411edb596eeeeac1e1edab6d8b25a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord told Enos that his sins were forgiven. Enos felt very happy. Enos was blessed because of his faith in Jesus Christ.

[Enos 1:3, 5–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng&id=p3,p5-p8#p3)

---

![Enos praying at night](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/45ebbdd8ae4411ed8ca4eeeeac1ec864181d791e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Enos wanted the Nephites to feel this happiness too. He kept praying. He asked the Lord for the Nephites to feel the same joy.

[Enos 1:9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng&id=p9#p9)

---

![Enos looking happy](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4631c78bae4411ed983deeeeac1ee9931d82cb79/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord told Enos that He would be with the Nephites if they kept His commandments. When Enos heard this, his faith in the Lord became even stronger.

[Enos 1:10–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng&id=p10-p11#p10)

---

![Enos writing on gold plates](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/466c116aae4411edbd3eeeeeac1ea3caabe6ec5e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Enos prayed again. He asked the Lord to bless the Lamanites and to keep the writing on the plates safe. He wanted the Lamanites to be able to read the plates and believe in the Lord. The Lord promised Enos that the Lamanites would read the writings one day.

[Enos 1:11–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/enos/1?lang=eng&id=p11-p17#p11)
