Escaping from the Lamanites

[Mosiah 19–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng)

---

![Gideon holds a sword and stands near a tower, and Noah is in the tower and points at a Lamanite army](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f3e0aaf2b70911ee9a20eeeeac1eee071d7e012a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

King Noah ruled a group of Nephites. He did many bad things, and some people were mad at him. A man named Gideon fought Noah with a sword. Noah ran away and climbed a tower. From the tower, he saw an army of Lamanites coming. Noah pretended he was afraid for his people, so Gideon let him live.

[Mosiah 11:1–2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/11?lang=eng&id=p1-p2#p1); [19:2–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p2-p8#p2)

---

![Noah runs away, and Noah’s people are scared](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f3a835c5b70911eeb5d0eeeeac1e616ead1304a9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Noah and his people ran away. But the Lamanites chased them and began attacking them. Noah told the men to leave their families behind and go with him.

[Mosiah 19:9–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p9-p11#p9)

---

![Limhi, Gideon, and other Nephites face the Lamanite army](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f3550ccbb70911eea288eeeeac1e593748bff9cc/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Some men left with Noah. But many of the men chose to stay with their families. Noah’s son Limhi also chose to stay.

[Mosiah 19:12, 16–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p12,p16-p17#p12)

---

![women stand in front of Limhi and the other Nephites](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f326d0dbb70911eebb22eeeeac1ee28d552d1fe4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Many daughters stood in front of the army and asked the Lamanites not to hurt their families. The Lamanites listened to the daughters and let the Nephites live. Instead, the Lamanites captured the Nephites.

[Mosiah 19:13–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p13-p15#p13)

---

![Noah and his men are angry with each other](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f2f5aeb4b70911eea002eeeeac1ef2a899a93b47/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The men who ran away wanted to go back to their families. Noah tried to stop them, so the men put him to death. Then they went back to their families.

[Mosiah 19:18–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p18-p22#p18)

---

![the Nephite men greet their families and Gideon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f2a45a5cb70911eea0adeeeeac1eb657bb40bf60/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The men were very happy that their families were safe. They told Gideon what happened to Noah.

[Mosiah 19:22–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p22-p24#p22)

---

![Limhi gives the Lamanites food and animals](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f287d1a8b70911ee8943eeeeac1e54fa5fca2e15/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The people chose Limhi to be their new king. Limhi promised the Lamanite king that they would pay the Lamanites half of everything they owned. In return, the Lamanite king promised he would not hurt Limhi’s people.

[Mosiah 19:25–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p25-p27#p25)

---

![Limhi and his wife look sad, and many Nephites are hurt](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f278dd8fb70911eebc6ceeeeac1e9d7b10d987a7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

They lived in peace for several years. Then the Lamanites started to treat Limhi’s people badly. The people wanted to be free again. They tried to fight the Lamanites, but they lost. The people prayed to God for help.

[Mosiah 19:29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/19?lang=eng&id=p29#p29); [21:1–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/21?lang=eng&id=p1-p15#p1)

---

![Limhi and Ammon talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f1e8846bb70911eea0adeeeeac1eb65758db228c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, a Nephite named Ammon visited Limhi and his people. Ammon came from a land called Zarahemla. Limhi was happy to see Ammon.

[Mosiah 21:22–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/21?lang=eng&id=p22-p24#p22)

---

![Gideon, Limhi, Ammon, and other Nephites talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f19163c0b70911ee8ff3eeeeac1e4ebb845e0887/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ammon could lead Limhi’s people to Zarahemla, but they had to escape from the Lamanites first. Gideon had a plan.

[Mosiah 21:36](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/21?lang=eng&id=p36#p36); [22:1–9, 11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/22?lang=eng&id=p1-p9,p11#p1)

---

![Lamanite guards sleep, and the Nephites leave the city](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f1578f1ab70911eebb22eeeeac1ee28d39607cde/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

At night, Gideon gave the Lamanite guards extra wine to make them fall asleep. While the guards slept, Limhi and all of his people escaped the city.

[Mosiah 22:7, 10–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/22?lang=eng&id=p7,p10-p12#p7)

---

![King Mosiah and his people welcome Limhi and his people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f149ac5cb70911ee87d5eeeeac1e2925cde5fb3b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

They went to Zarahemla and joined the Nephites there. Limhi and his people learned more about God. They made a covenant, or special promise, to serve God and obey His commandments. They were baptized and became part of God’s Church. They remembered that God helped them escape from the Lamanites.

[Mosiah 21:32–35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/21?lang=eng&id=p32-p35#p32); [22:13–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/22?lang=eng&id=p13-p14#p13); [25:16–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/25?lang=eng&id=p16-p18#p16)
