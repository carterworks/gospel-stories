Preparing to go to the promised land

[1 Nephi 17–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng)

---

![families by the sea](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/08f4f1cdb23311edbfe9eeeeac1e325f318cc6d8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Bountiful was a land filled with fruits and honey. It was a beautiful place to live. Lehi and Sariah’s family lived by the sea and had everything they needed.

[1 Nephi 17:5–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p5-p6#p5)

---

![Nephi praying](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/091b6596b23311edaabbeeeeac1ea78c940fa9c6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After many days, the Lord told Nephi to go to a mountain to pray. There, the Lord told him to build a ship to take his family across the sea.

[1 Nephi 17:7–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p7-p8#p7)

---

![Nephi looking at rocks](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/09477ea5b23311ed98c1eeeeac1ea02b970405e7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord showed Nephi how to build the ship. But Nephi did not know where to find metal to make tools. The Lord showed Nephi where to look for metal.

[1 Nephi 17:8–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p8-p10#p8)

---

![Nephi working while Laman and Lemuel talk](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/09b338c7b23311edb64aeeeeac1ea81bbf3dc464/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Later, Nephi built a fire to make tools. Laman and Lemuel watched their brother Nephi. They did not want to help him build the ship. They thought it was a bad idea to try to cross the sea.

[1 Nephi 17:11, 16–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p11,p16-p18#p11)

---

![Laman and Lemuel mad at Nephi](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/09e6f2fbb23311ed92eeeeeeac1eb1f840aeae27/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Laman and Lemuel did not believe that the Lord told Nephi to build a ship. Nephi asked them why they still did not have faith in the Lord. He reminded his brothers that they had seen an angel and knew of the Lord’s power. Laman and Lemuel became so mad that they wanted to kill Nephi.

[1 Nephi 17:18–19, 45–48](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p18-p19,p45-p48#p18)

---

![Laman and Lemuel on the ground](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0a3b2d6db23311eda14aeeeeac1e3ee4ccb0cc1a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But Nephi was filled with God’s power. Nephi warned Laman and Lemuel not to touch him. They were afraid and did not dare to touch Nephi for many days. Then the Lord told Nephi to reach out his hand to his brothers. When Nephi reached out to them, they felt shaken by the Lord’s power.

[1 Nephi 17:48, 52–55](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/17?lang=eng&id=p48,p52-p55#p48)

---

![everyone working on the ship](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0a8e2f76b23311ed98c1eeeeac1ea02b6209c508/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Laman and Lemuel worshipped the Lord and helped build the ship. Nephi prayed to the Lord many times for help. The ship that Nephi’s family built was beautiful. After many days, the ship was finished, and they saw that it was good. Nephi’s family knew the Lord had helped them.

[1 Nephi 18:1–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/18?lang=eng&id=p1-p4#p1)
