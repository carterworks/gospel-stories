Finding joy in Jesus Christ

[Alma 18–19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/18?lang=eng)

---

![queen’s husband falling to the floor](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9b210db3379a11ee9a50eeeeac1e1a3813b27cc3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A Lamanite queen ruled with her husband, King Lamoni. The king asked a Nephite named Ammon to teach him about the Lord. The king believed what Ammon told him. The king knelt to pray and asked the Lord to forgive him. As he prayed, the king fell to the earth. It looked like he was dead.

[Alma 18:21, 24–42](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/18?lang=eng&id=p21,p24-p42#p21)

---

![queen looking at her fallen husband](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9ad0a3c6379a11eea824eeeeac1e1922a795b099/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The king’s servants took him to the queen. They put him on his bed. He lay there and did not move for two days and two nights.

[Alma 18:43](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/18?lang=eng&id=p43#p43)

---

![queen talking to servant](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9a887735379a11eea322eeeeac1e892857bc4f15/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

During that time, the queen and her children were very sad. They stayed with the king and cried over him. Some people said the king should be buried. But the queen wanted to talk to Ammon first. She heard that he had God’s power. The queen’s servants asked Ammon to come to the queen.

[Alma 18:43](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/18?lang=eng&id=p43#p43); [19:1–3, 5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/19?lang=eng&id=p1-p3,p5#p1)

---

![queen talking to Ammon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9a2d10c5379a11eea824eeeeac1e192240149299/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The queen told Ammon she heard he was a prophet of God. She asked Ammon to go see the king. Ammon knew the king was alive. It was God’s power that caused the king to sleep. Ammon told the queen that her husband would wake up the next day.

[Alma 19:3–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/19?lang=eng&id=p3-p8#p3)

---

![queen resting her head on her husband](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/99f14046379a11ee9764eeeeac1e0cc3053e74d7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The queen trusted Ammon and had great faith in God. She believed her husband would wake up the next day. Ammon said she was blessed because of her great faith. He told the queen she had more faith than his own people had. The queen stayed close and watched over her husband all night.

[Alma 19:9–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/19?lang=eng&id=p9-p11#p9)

---

![queen happy her husband woke up](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/99d8af34379a11ee889deeeeac1e4f466b2d8eef/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The next day, the king woke up. He told the queen that he had seen Jesus Christ. The queen and king knew how much Jesus loved them, and they were full of joy.

[Alma 19:12–13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/19?lang=eng&id=p12-p13#p12)
