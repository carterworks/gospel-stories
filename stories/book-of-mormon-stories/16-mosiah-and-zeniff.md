Protected by the Lord

[Omni 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng); [Mosiah 9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/9?lang=eng)

---

![Mosiah leading people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8b8eab242ff511eea5f5eeeeac1e570e7f55fa06/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Nephites and Lamanites had many wars. One day, the Lord told a Nephite named Mosiah to leave the land of Nephi with anyone who would follow the Lord.

[Omni 1:10, 12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng&id=p10,p12#p10)

---

![Mosiah looking at city](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8af5c6922ff511eeb656eeeeac1e226550a9c3e5/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Many Nephites obeyed the Lord and left with Mosiah. The Lord led them to a land that had people living in it. They were called the people of Zarahemla.

[Omni 1:13–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng&id=p13-p14#p13)

---

![Mosiah talking to people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8aebb46e2ff511eeb99deeeeac1ed2a80ffbf73f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The people of Zarahemla also came from Jerusalem long ago. They were happy the Lord sent the Nephites with the brass plates. Mosiah’s people joined the people of Zarahemla. All the people chose Mosiah as their king. He taught them about the Lord.

[Omni 1:14–19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng&id=p14-p19#p14)

---

![Zeniff leading people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8ac5b5d92ff511eebfbbeeeeac1e2a283dc6b4ac/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Nephites had been living in Zarahemla for some time when a large group went back to the land of Nephi. They were led by a Nephite named Zeniff.

[Omni 1:27–29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng&id=p27-p29#p27); [Mosiah 9:3–5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/9?lang=eng&id=p3-p5#p3)

---

![Lamanite king talking](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8a3869fe2ff511ee9f3ceeeeac1ef64e18e55122/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lamanites now lived in the land of Nephi, so Zeniff asked their king if his people could live there too. The king agreed.

[Mosiah 9:6–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/9?lang=eng&id=p6-p10#p6)

---

![Lamanites attacking](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8a21fbc72ff511ee8a02eeeeac1ead7af5cb6bf1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The king tricked Zeniff and his people. He let them live in the land of Nephi so he could take some of their food and animals later. Zeniff’s people lived there in peace for years. They grew a lot of food and had many animals. Then the Lamanites attacked and tried to take their food and animals.

[Mosiah 9:10–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/9?lang=eng&id=p10-p14#p10)

---

![Zeniff’s people winning fight](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8a1f8ace2ff511eebb85eeeeac1ea2e875db710b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Zeniff taught his people to trust the Lord. When the Lamanites came to fight them, Zeniff and his people prayed to the Lord. The Lord gave them strength and helped protect them. They were able to chase the Lamanites away. The Lord blessed Zeniff and his people for their faith.

[Mosiah 9:15–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/9?lang=eng&id=p15-p18#p15)
