Called to free the Lord’s people

Exodus 2–3

---

![Egyptian beating Israelite](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/185927284e1d9982367c6144a65f4d7dc938f678/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Moses grew up as a son of Pharaoh’s daughter. Moses saw that the Egyptians were mean to the Israelites. He was sad that the Egyptians made the Israelites their slaves.

[Exodus 2:10–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/2?lang=eng&id=p10-p11#p10)

---

![Moses defending Israelite](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/870a9594046fa71ec8fc408fca31d40c2c15d73e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day Moses saw an Egyptian hitting an Israelite. To defend the Israelite, Moses killed the Egyptian.

[Exodus 2:11–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/2?lang=eng&id=p11-p14#p11)

---

![Moses in wilderness](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/d32e5ff1703ec92c9ee53ac73ccb273e136dd045/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Pharaoh found out, he wanted to kill Moses, but Moses ran away from Egypt.

[Exodus 2:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/2?lang=eng&id=p15#p15)

---

![Moses and family](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8fd9ce9b69c91e49fb8af6efba4cf415dfa3228f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Moses came to a place called Midian, where he met a woman named Zipporah. They got married and had children.

[Exodus 2:21–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/2?lang=eng&id=p21-p22#p21)

---

![Moses looking at burning bush](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bd11b40b88473d7671a3a208fabe356b05628864/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

While living in Midian, Moses saw a bush that was on fire, but the fire did not burn up the bush. The Lord appeared in the fire and spoke to Moses.

[Exodus 3:1–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/3?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1)

---

![Jesus talking to Moses](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4d65819f5b1eb5eb8e909f2ab423571baebc9931/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord said He knew the Israelites were suffering in Egypt. He commanded Moses to return to Egypt and to tell Pharaoh to let the Israelites go free. The Lord said He would help Moses free the Israelites and lead them to a promised land.

[Exodus 3:7–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/3?lang=eng&id=p7-p18#p7)
