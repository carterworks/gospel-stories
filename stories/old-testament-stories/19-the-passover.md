Protected by the Lord

[Exodus 11–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/11?lang=eng); [14–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/14?lang=eng)

---

![Moses praying](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7c076208d3d711ebb6b2eeeeac1e682fba37d421/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Pharaoh would not let the Israelites go free, so the Lord told Moses that He would send one last plague. The firstborn child of every family in the land of Egypt would die, even the firstborn of their animals.

[Exodus 11:1, 4–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/11?lang=eng&id=p1,4-p10#p1)

---

![Moses talking to people](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9c8fa157d3d711eb9c60eeeeac1ed7da51c05e74/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord promised that if the Israelites followed His instructions, the plague would pass over them and not hurt them.

[Exodus 12:3, 13, 23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p3,13,23#p3)

---

![Man painting doorframe, children watching](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c7385870d3d711ebabf2eeeeac1ebabb28f246db/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord told each Israelite family to sacrifice a perfect male lamb and paint the blood of the lamb on the doorframe of their houses.

[Exodus 12:4–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p4-p7#p4)

---

![Family eating dinner](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/08a3ef3cd3d811eba535eeeeac1e0d35504c299f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord told the Israelites to cook and quickly eat the lamb. While they ate, they should be dressed and ready to leave their homes. The Lord said if the Israelites did these things, their firstborn would be safe from the plague.

[Exodus 12:8–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p8-p11#p8)

---

![Pharaoh’s son dead](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2ab4f169d3d811ebaef0eeeeac1e3e2c066d51e1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Like the Lord warned, the plague came. All the firstborn in Egypt died, including Pharaoh’s oldest son. But the plague passed over each home that had lamb’s blood on the doorframe. The firstborn of the Israelites were saved because they obeyed the Lord.

[Exodus 12:12–13, 29–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p12-p13,29-p30#p12)

---

![sad Pharaoh telling Moses and Aaron to leave](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6ee685f4d3d811eb8eeaeeeeac1e597d43f13de5/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Pharaoh saw his own son was dead because of this plague, he told Moses and Aaron to take all the Israelites and leave Egypt.

[Exodus 12:31–33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p31-p33#p31)

---

![angry Pharaoh sending army](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/98d82219d3d811ebb9d8eeeeac1e734981aa99f6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Israelites left, but Pharaoh was angry. He gathered his army and chariots and chased after the Israelites.

[Exodus 12:37–41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p37-p41#p37); [14:5–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/14?lang=eng&id=p5-p8#p5)

---

![army chasing Israelites](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/be0afdb1d3d811ebabf2eeeeac1ebabb2221f912/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Israelites camped at the Red Sea. Soon Pharaoh and his army caught up to them. When the Israelites saw the Egyptians coming, they were afraid. But Moses told the Israelites that the Lord would protect them.

[Exodus 14:9–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/14?lang=eng&id=p9-p14#p9)

---

![Moses lifting rod, Red Sea parting](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e720b8b5d3d811eb8eeaeeeeac1e597df168e70f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

As the Egyptians got closer, the Lord told Moses to lift up his rod. Moses did, and the Lord parted the sea. The Israelites crossed the sea on dry ground. They ran away from Pharaoh and his army.

[Exodus 14:15–16, 21–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/14?lang=eng&id=p15-p16,21-p22#p15)

---

![Red Sea falling on Egyptian army](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1848cc2ad3d911eb82bceeeeac1e935673c8f97f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Egyptian army chased after the Israelites. When all the Israelites were safe on the other side of the sea, the Lord let the water come down. The Egyptian army drowned in the sea.

[Exodus 14:23–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/14?lang=eng&id=p23-p30#p23)

---

![Israelites dancing](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5020c1d8d3d911eb9c60eeeeac1ed7da1c53d14a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Israelites were free at last. They sang songs, danced, and thanked the Lord. They always remembered the Passover as the time the Lord saved their lives and led them out of Egypt.

[Exodus 14:31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/14?lang=eng&id=p31#p31); [15:1–22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/15?lang=eng&id=p1-p22#p1)
