A promise to bless the human family

Genesis 11–15; 17; Abraham 1–2

---

![angel saves Abraham from priest](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ba83c586bcf32151d43f3ec484b898739d3e735c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham lived in the city of Ur. Wicked priests there wanted to sacrifice him to their idols. Abraham prayed, and the Lord saved him.

[Abraham 1:1–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/1?lang=eng&id=p1-p20#p1)

---

![Abraham and Sarah travel with camel](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b0d2d7ba872b39336ec3643039fd87882f82f60f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then the Lord commanded Abraham and his wife Sarah to leave Ur and travel to a faraway land. He promised to bless them on their journey.

[Genesis 12:1–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/12?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1); [Abraham 2:2–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/2?lang=eng&id=p2-p4#p2)

---

![Abraham and Sarah caring for sheep](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c15c7b29cd8d732a93ea78c6e046bb805f51840c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham and Sarah trusted the Lord and left Ur. But they felt sad because they couldn’t have children. The Lord comforted them. He promised that they would have a child.

[Genesis 11:30–31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/11?lang=eng&id=p30-p31#p30); [15:1–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/15?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1); [17:2–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/17?lang=eng&id=p2-p16#p2); [Abraham 2:6–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/2?lang=eng&id=p6-p9#p6)

---

![God appears to Abraham](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2c2a2a607c4821b9b052ea009dd6d1c32c44ebc4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham prayed to the Lord to learn more about Him. The Lord visited Abraham and called Himself Jehovah. Jehovah made a covenant with Abraham. He promised that Abraham would have the priesthood. He also promised that through Abraham’s family, all the families of the earth would be blessed.

[Abraham 2:6–11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/2?lang=eng&id=p6-p11#p6)

---

![Abraham and Sarah travel with camels](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c5afc9bb216d6a712f6f1859fb4718083cf7fe99/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

As Abraham and Sarah traveled, they needed to find food. They tried to live in a land called Canaan. There was no food there, so they had to go to Egypt. But living in Egypt was dangerous for them.

[Genesis 12:10–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/12?lang=eng&id=p10-p20#p10); [Abraham 2:21–25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/2?lang=eng&id=p21-p25#p21)

---

![Abraham and Sarah look at animals](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/20794be8ecb3f242175206ea57aa98ad83c2599b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham and Sarah left Egypt and returned to live in Canaan. They brought food and animals from Egypt with them. Canaan was part of the land the Lord promised them.

[Genesis 13:1–4, 12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/13?lang=eng&id=p1-p4,12#p1); [Abraham 2:19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/2?lang=eng&id=p19#p19)

---

![Melchizedek talking to Abraham and Sarah](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5355d57d1524025d5ab1038a6b9ba28e1aa8250a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Lord also kept His promise that Abraham would receive the priesthood. One day Abraham and Sarah met a righteous king named Melchizedek. Abraham paid tithing to him.

[Genesis 14:18–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/14?lang=eng&id=p18-p24#p18); [Alma 13:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/13?lang=eng&id=p15#p15)

---

![Melchizedek blessing Abraham](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0ce00e3099c34e99f599972becef8054d85f40a8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham received the priesthood from Melchizedek. This was the same priesthood that the prophets Adam and Noah had received.

[Joseph Smith Translation, Genesis 14:36–40](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/jst/jst-gen/14?lang=eng&id=p36-p40#p36); [Abraham 1:2–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/1?lang=eng&id=p2-p4#p2); [Doctrine and Covenants 84:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/84?lang=eng&id=p14#p14)

---

![Abraham and Sarah](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c37a78ef301810656c7319eb4f7dea393a1bfeb8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham and Sarah were happy in Canaan, but they still worried because they didn’t have a child. They had to trust the Lord’s promise that one day their family would grow and bless the whole earth.

[Genesis 13:12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/13?lang=eng&id=p12#p12); [15:3–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/15?lang=eng&id=p3-p6#p3)
