A slave becomes a leader

[Genesis 39–41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng)

---

![Joseph sold to Potiphar](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/33c43447d3c011ebaef0eeeeac1e3e2ce9492536/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph was sold as a slave to a man named Potiphar. Potiphar worked for Pharaoh, the ruler of Egypt. Potiphar could tell that the Lord helped Joseph. He trusted Joseph and put him in charge of his house and everything he owned.

[Genesis 39:1–6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng&id=p1-p6#p1)

---

![Joseph telling Potiphar’s wife no](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/60b8b014d3c011eb82bceeeeac1e93567ebc987e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Potiphar’s wife liked Joseph. She wanted Joseph to break the Lord’s commandments with her. Joseph told her no.

[Genesis 39:7–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng&id=p7-p10#p7)

---

![Joseph running from Potiphar’s wife](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a0989de8d3c011ebaef0eeeeac1e3e2c99a01889/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Potiphar’s wife would not listen, so Joseph ran away. She was angry at Joseph.

[Genesis 39:11–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng&id=p11-p12#p11)

---

![Potiphar’s wife talking to Potiphar](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c4a5be13d3c011ebb6b2eeeeac1e682fed31485a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

She showed Potiphar a piece of Joseph’s clothing. She lied to Potiphar about Joseph. Potiphar put Joseph in prison.

[Genesis 39:13–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng&id=p13-p20#p13)

---

![Joseph in prison](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/eb102f45d3c011eb82bceeeeac1e9356a5f2bdcc/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph had been separated from his family. He had become a slave, and now he was a prisoner. But the Lord still helped Joseph. Joseph did not give up. The Lord blessed the prison guard to see the good in Joseph. The guard began to trust him, so he put Joseph in charge of the other prisoners.

[Genesis 39:21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng&id=p21#p21)

---

![Joseph interpreting prisoners’ dreams](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1443847dd3c111ebabf2eeeeac1ebabb1b037c2c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph met two prisoners, a baker and a butler, who had worked for Pharaoh. They both had strange dreams. Through the Lord’s power, Joseph explained what their dreams meant. The butler’s dream meant that the butler would be freed. Three days later, he was set free to work for Pharaoh again.

[Genesis 39:22–23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/39?lang=eng&id=p22-p23#p22); [40:1–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/40?lang=eng&id=p1-p21#p1)

---

![Pharaoh upset](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/53c99266d3c111eb82bceeeeac1e935652e1d52a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day Pharaoh was upset by his dreams. No one could tell him what his dreams meant.

[Genesis 41:1–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/41?lang=eng&id=p1-p8#p1)

---

![butler speaking to Pharaoh](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/78bb1f3ad3c111eba535eeeeac1e0d35a1a283ac/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then the butler remembered that Joseph could explain dreams.

[Genesis 41:9–13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/41?lang=eng&id=p9-p13#p9)

---

![Joseph interpreting Pharaoh’s dreams](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/95ced129d3c111eb9e65eeeeac1e40722e778c5a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph was brought out of prison to explain Pharaoh’s dreams. Joseph said the dreams meant that Egypt would have seven years with lots of food followed by seven years of famine with very little food. Joseph told Pharaoh that Egypt should save extra food during the good years.

[Genesis 41:14–36](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/41?lang=eng&id=p14-p36#p14)

---

![Joseph showing Pharaoh food storage](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bcf73b24d3c111ebb6b2eeeeac1e682fdc611b47/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Pharaoh knew that what Joseph said about his dreams was true. He set Joseph free from prison and made Joseph a great leader in Egypt. For seven years, Joseph helped Egypt store extra food.

[Genesis 41:37–53](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/41?lang=eng&id=p37-p53#p37)

---

![people traveling in Egypt](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e9d1ed79d3c111ebaf3ceeeeac1e793138e2cf38/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then the famine came. During this time, no one could grow any food. People traveled to Egypt to buy the food Joseph had stored. Because of Joseph, the Egyptians saved enough food to help them and others survive the famine.

[Genesis 41:54–57](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/41?lang=eng&id=p54-p57#p54)
