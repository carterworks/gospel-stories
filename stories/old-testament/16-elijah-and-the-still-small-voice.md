![Elijah walking up a mountain](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/47278e0f8ba911ec9adbeeeeac1eca4e8a28c435/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Elijah was a prophet. He wanted to hear God’s voice. He went to a mountain to hear God’s voice better.

---

![Elijah watching rocks and trees blow](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4c9ca66f8ba911eca505eeeeac1ed7099ad7ba88/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

There was a strong wind. It was so strong that it broke rocks. The wind was loud. But it was not God’s voice.

---

![flames from a fire](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/530fcda48ba911eca354eeeeac1effb7fee840db/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Next there was an earthquake. The earth shook. There was a fire too. It had big flames. But the earthquake and the fire were not God’s voice.

---

![Elijah watching a peaceful sunset](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/56b6dc988ba911ec9b81eeeeac1e59a8c4e8da68/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then it was quiet. Elijah heard God’s voice. It was still and small. But it was clear.

---

![children sitting in sacrament meeting](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/dd134cf19e0f11ec8e79eeeeac1e7efe47363ab0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can hear God’s voice by listening to the Holy Ghost. He speaks to me in small and simple ways.
