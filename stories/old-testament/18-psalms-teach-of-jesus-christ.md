Psalms are sacred songs. People in the Bible wrote psalms to praise God. Many of their psalms teach about Jesus Christ. Here’s what some of the psalms from the Bible teach.

---

![Jesus calming a storm](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ec4612819e0611eca23aeeeeac1e127ac7bf5745/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

*I love the Lord. He is my rock and my strength. I will trust Him. I will praise Him.*

---

![Jesus holding a small lamb](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ec5b6f429e0611eca23aeeeeac1e127ad059fbb6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

*The Lord is my Shepherd. He leads me to green meadows. He leads me to still waters. I will not fear.*

---

![Jesus holding up a light](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/edf12a749e0611ecb814eeeeac1e5e2ed8fb5edf/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

*The Lord is my light. I will not be afraid. I will have courage. He will make my heart strong.*

---

![Primary children singing at church](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ef17f7289e0611ec836eeeeeac1e6db9c3e1c635/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I love Jesus Christ, and He loves me. I can praise Him when I learn and sing about Him.
