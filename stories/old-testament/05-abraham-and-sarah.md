You can read the story of Abraham and Sarah in [Genesis 15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/15?lang=eng), [17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/17?lang=eng), and [21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/21?lang=eng).

---

![Abraham and Sarah kneeling together](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2cc7d9536e7b11eca910eeeeac1e44121e89e198/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham was a prophet. God made a covenant with Abraham and his wife, Sarah.

---

![Abraham and Sarah kneeling in their tent](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2bda92fe6e7b11ec9733eeeeac1e92c43536cde6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

God promised to bless them. He told them they would have children. Abraham and Sarah promised to follow God.

---

![Abraham and Sarah looking sad](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/293da1146e7b11ec9a71eeeeac1e7566d61065be/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Abraham and Sarah kept their promise. But they did not have any children for a long time. They got older and older.

---

![Abraham and Sarah holding a baby](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/28af43b96e7b11ecae56eeeeac1eb20f191afab9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Finally, when Abraham was 100 and Sarah was 90, they had a baby boy. They named him Isaac. They knew God had blessed them.

---

![young girl planting seeds](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2b3f64976e7b11ec9419eeeeac1eed194ffe1f68/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can trust God. Sometimes blessings don’t come right away. But God always keeps His promises.
