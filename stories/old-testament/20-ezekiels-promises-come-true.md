![Ezekiel writing on a scroll](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8885c566c39e11ec9fdeeeeeac1ecb9785359cd3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ezekiel was a prophet. God showed him many things in the future that hadn’t happened yet. Ezekiel wrote them down.

---

![An illustration of the Bible and the Book of Mormon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/887d39e4c39e11ec8e0deeeeac1eb4d5407d58da/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ezekiel wrote about two books that would be printed someday. One book was the Bible. The other book was the Book of Mormon.

---

![Man looking at papers next to a printing press.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/88a49800c39e11ec87a8eeeeac1e352d09b1faff/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Many years later, the Bible was printed.

---

![Emma Smith transcribing the Book of Mormon for Joseph.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/88ec013ec39e11ec8f27eeeeac1e733af25a25e3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After many more years, Joseph Smith translated the Book of Mormon. Ezekiel’s vision came true!

---

![Jesus Christ holding a shepherd’s crook.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8e6c1600c39e11ec974beeeeac1eb513f2b59d7e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Today we can read the Bible and the Book of Mormon. They work together to teach about Jesus Christ.

---

![A family helping a little girl read the scriptures](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/8f0ac701c39e11ecb8f3eeeeac1e058cd1034510/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When I read the scriptures, I learn about Jesus Christ.
