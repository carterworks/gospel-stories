After Moses died, the Lord chose Joshua to become the prophet. God told Joshua to be strong and have courage.

---

![men in white carrying chest across riverbed](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fc0641d289d511eca6dfeeeeac1e6f0a2368f41c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joshua led the people to the promised land. When they crossed the River Jordan, God stopped the water so they could walk across.

---

![Joshua teaching](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fde64c7989d511eca505eeeeac1ed7098fd8b1c8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joshua taught people about choices. He said they could choose whether or not to follow God.

---

![many people sitting and watching Joshua teach](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/02444f3889d611ecb9e7eeeeac1e43a952762ca2/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joshua chose to follow God. Many of the people did too.

---

![Primary children walking behind Primary teacher](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/01d7aaee89d611eca83ceeeeac1eafb1ff112a89/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can have courage like Joshua. I can choose to follow God.
