You can read this story in [Genesis 41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/gen/41?lang=eng).

---

![Joseph speaking to the pharaoh in Egypt](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f54aa824806711ec8ee0eeeeac1ea01c522953b4/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph was a prophet. He lived in Egypt. One night Pharaoh, the king of Egypt, had a strange dream. He asked Joseph what the dream meant.

---

![young boy holding out empty plate](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f5711bee806711ec855feeeeac1e1786cdad1ee1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

God helped Joseph understand the dream. For seven years, people would have plenty of food. Then for seven years, they wouldn’t have enough. Joseph told Pharaoh.

---

![Joseph helping save grain](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f544dbcc806711ec85e7eeeeac1eb2ee05b501ec/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Joseph said they should save food now. Then they would be ready for hard times. Pharaoh put Joseph in charge of saving food. Joseph worked hard.

---

![Joseph helping hand out bags of food](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f55e7e34806711ec80d4eeeeac1ec5fe46757ccb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the seven years of famine came, the people had enough food to eat. They even had enough to share with others.

---

![children walking to church and holding umbrella in rain](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/f92a2c3a806711eca2d5eeeeac1e241ed725f05d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can prepare now. With God’s help, I can get through hard times!
