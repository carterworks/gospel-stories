Many prophets taught about Jesus Christ. They said He would be born to show us how to live. They used many names to teach about Him.

---

![Jesus, as a boy, is learning about carpentry from Joseph.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ff9f88a1e96011ec8827eeeeac1e6b9609efe949/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Sometimes in the scriptures Jesus is called **Immanuel.** That name means “God with us.”

---

![Illustration depicting Jesus Christ sitting beside the daughter of Jairus and holding her hand as he heals her while her parents watch in faith.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ecc3ce27e96011eca3f4eeeeac1e7ddc5cb46bb0/full/%21640%2C/0/default) ![This image is 6 6 spot illustrations from the life of Jesus Christ. 1. Another name for Jesus is the **Prince of Peace.** When we are scared or upset, He can help us feel peace through the Holy Ghost.

---

![Jesus Christ praying in the garden of Gethsemane.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ecc1ab3ae96011ecbe03eeeeac1ecc2357950e76/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus is also called the **Messiah.** *Messiah* means “the anointed one.” Jesus died for us so we can live with God again.

---

![Jesus asked Heavenly Father to forgive the men who crucified Him because they didn’t know they were hurting the Son  of God.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ecbffda0e96011eca877eeeeac1eda9c28f5c3ad/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus is our **Savior.** He saves us from our sins and death.

---

![A boy and girl visit with their Grandfather inside a nursing home. The older man is sitting in a wheelchair and is reading the scriptures to his grandchildren.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/af74eb90e96011ecacc5eeeeac1e17ba8b1d5f1b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I love Jesus Christ. I can learn about His life and love in the scriptures.
