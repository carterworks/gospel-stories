![Heavenly Father and Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/61d3d9ff4bda11ec80e1eeeeac1e58a30b6753a9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Before we were born, we lived in heaven with our Heavenly Father. He taught us about His plan of happiness.

---

![boy looking sadly at girl holding soccer ball](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5f13cfa94bda11ecbc95eeeeac1e522ab302606c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Heavenly Father said we would come to earth to get bodies. We would learn and make choices. Sometimes we would make mistakes. We would need a Savior.

---

![girl holding out soccer ball to boy](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5e980ff84bda11ecbc95eeeeac1e522ad22fa082/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Savior would show us how to live. And when we made a wrong choice, we would be able to repent.

---

![Jesus standing with arms outstretched in front of earth](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5e37526a4bda11ec8634eeeeac1eadf4d98e4e39/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus said, “Here am I, send me.” Heavenly Father chose Him to be our Savior. Jesus promised to come to earth to save us.

---

![Jesus and Heavenly Father standing in beautiful garden](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5d195f484bda11ec8634eeeeac1eadf48416b2d3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can follow Jesus. Someday I can go back and live with Heavenly Father.
