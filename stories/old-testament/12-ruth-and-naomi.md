Naomi and her family came to a new land because of a famine. They met a young woman named Ruth. Ruth married Naomi’s son.

---

![man laying sick in bed](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a144009a8a0511eca59aeeeeac1eb91f1b9580ed/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Ruth’s husband died. Ruth was very sad.

---

![older woman in blue scarf, looking sad](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a12e7cc78a0511ec9b81eeeeac1e59a885e00f23/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Naomi was also very sad. She decided to go back to the land she came from.

---

![Ruth and Naomi talking with sunset behind them](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a7956f328a0511eca83ceeeeac1eafb152681607/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Naomi told Ruth to go back to her family. But Ruth loved Naomi. She promised to come with Naomi and take care of her.

---

![Ruth gathering basket of grain](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/a783467d8a0511ec8827eeeeac1ee76dac1a077c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Ruth and Naomi were poor. So Ruth worked hard to find food for them.

---

![Boaz talking to Ruth in a wheat field](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/abe8c3898a0511ecb9e7eeeeac1e43a9d8b1835d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day a man named Boaz saw Ruth gathering leftover grain in one of his fields.

---

![Boaz talking to man in field](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ad8bec598a0511ec9adbeeeeac1eca4ef75f86e8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Boaz was kind. He told his workers to leave extra food for Ruth.

---

![Ruth, Boaz, and Naomi looking at new baby](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/afed5bda8a0511ecb9e7eeeeac1e43a91cdd97f6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Later Ruth and Boaz got married. They had a baby boy. Naomi helped take care of him.

---

![girl helping serve dinner in backyard](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b035d6ab8a0511eca59aeeeeac1eb91fa382c57b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can help take care of others. I can be kind to those in need.
