Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego were good friends. They helped each other choose the right.

---

![King Nebuchadnezzar II demands that everybody bow down and worship a graven image.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1551d5ead52a11ecbcc8eeeeac1ea86e6f1d43c3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day the king told everyone to pray to a golden statue. If they didn’t, he would throw them into a fire.

---

![Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego refuse to bow down and worship the graven image.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1f5d177dd52a11ec9688eeeeac1eb0625e437c0f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Everyone else prayed to the statue. But the three friends prayed only to God.

---

![Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego are thrown into the fiery furnace.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/234c7ab7d52a11ecbb6deeeeac1e1e17adf10e23/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The king was angry. He threw Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego into the fire.

---

![Jehovah appears to Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-nego in the fiery furnace and protects them from the consuming flames.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/803b6979d52b11eca030eeeeac1e26a9d8fc681f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But the fire didn’t burn them! A heavenly being stood with them and kept them safe.

---

![A young girl declines to look at something on a smartphone](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/864f0c2fd52b11ec9cd7eeeeac1ed7670c20816f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can choose the right, even when it’s hard. Jesus will help me and be with me!
