Isaiah was a prophet. He lived before Jesus Christ was born. But he knew Jesus would come.

---

![Isaiah speaking](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/65b31e7ba07f11ec8e79eeeeac1e7efe3b31041d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Isaiah said Jesus would be born as the Son of God. Jesus would help and heal others.

---

![Mary and Joseph with baby Jesus](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/65c6f487a07f11ec836eeeeeac1e6db9a72c433b/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Isaiah also said Jesus would come to earth. He would be called the Prince of Peace. He would give His life for us because He loves us so much.

---

![women crying over Jesus’s body](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fa4804e1c39511ecac48eeeeac1ebc0784c54ce4/full/%21640%2C/0/default) ![resurrected Jesus holding out His arms](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/6589eb9fa07f11ec9d66eeeeac1e88d3979f0da7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The things Isaiah taught did happen. Many years later, Jesus came to earth. He showed us the way. He died and lived again. He lives today!

---

![mom in bright clothes reading book about Jesus to children](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/65897654a07f11ec85c4eeeeac1ec3a6f13337d0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

I can learn about Jesus Christ. When I remember what He has done for me, I can feel peace and hope!
