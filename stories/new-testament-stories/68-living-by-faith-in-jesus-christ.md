He is “the author and finisher of our faith”

[Hebrews 11–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng)

---

![A man and a girl observe people destroying their pottery.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/bgf1tiuqf932rp71jbydzbaxtuqmhq8v65uaofy6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

It wasn’t always easy to be a follower of Jesus Christ in the early days of the Church. People sometimes hated Church members or made fun of them because of their beliefs. Paul wanted to help them keep their faith in Jesus Christ.

[Hebrews 10:32–39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/10?lang=eng&id=p32-p39#p32)

---

![The man and the girl praying, with Paul in the foreground.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/uvifv5gw5do2ghk3gdzxdua2uhmq9jfbwy9vwg7m/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul wrote a letter to the Hebrew, or Jewish, Saints. He taught that faith means to believe and hope for something, even though we can’t see it. For example, having faith in Jesus Christ means trusting Him and His promises, even if we haven’t received the blessings we hope for yet.

[Hebrews 11:1, 6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p1,p6#p1)

---

![The girl smiling as the man reads stories about people who had faith.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1j0dz5wtd3zgwtwh4awvieyzwhuzt9ojf6gkdvvr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

To help the Hebrew Saints face their challenges with faith, Paul reminded them of stories about people in the scriptures who had great faith.

[Hebrews 11:4–40](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p4-p40#p4)

---

![Noah looking at instructions for building the ark.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/k4fueg7n1wfh99oz5ch7qaltrzmyzu392z84zh4d/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

For example, Paul told about Noah. God told Noah to build an ark to save his family from a flood. Noah had faith to obey, even before he saw the rain.

[Hebrews 11:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p7#p7)

---

![Abraham and Sarah leaving their home.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/2gme9wsovq2mtx8vvxcwa349l8amkgp3vin8ozdd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul told about Abraham and Sarah, who had faith to leave their home because God promised to lead them to a better place. They also had faith in God’s promise that they would have a very large family, even though they were old and did not have children yet.

[Hebrews 11:8–16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p8-p16#p8)

---

![Moses’s mother and sister hiding him in a basket.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/kwr100jaig2jpaoqdxm645mpyr8561g4rhr8ty9y/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul also told about Moses’s mother, Jochebed. She had faith to hide Moses, when he was a baby, to keep him safe from an evil king.

[Hebrews 11:23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p23#p23)

---

![Moses observing an Egyptian mistreating an Israelite.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/xm7gaqlartpu17dpd8rpnmtlgetqijvlvd9wz0mi/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Moses grew up in Egypt. He could have been rich and comfortable. But he gave up all of that because he had faith in God and His blessings.

[Hebrews 11:24–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p24-p27#p24)

---

![Moses parting the Red Sea.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0vmv1wpq8g0rit5fad286r9ss2tv74xojlm18gxf/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Because of his faith, Moses led God’s people out of slavery. They even walked through the Red Sea on dry ground!

[Hebrews 11:29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p29#p29)

---

![Rahab with her children.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/cn3hzbesqnyw9x1l0hi0dnypw8awgn80b0i9oqrj/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul also told about Rahab. She had faith to help God’s people, even though no one else in her city believed in the Lord. Her faith saved her family.

[Hebrews 11:30–31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/11?lang=eng&id=p30-p31#p30)

---

![The man and the girl look at the pot they have repaired.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ict3cwgzkdu3gm2ij1dljf8ppvspetedg0gex7jr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Paul said that these many examples of faith can help us have faith in Jesus Christ too, even when life is hard. We can repent of our sins and look to Jesus, the “author and finisher of our faith.” He was willing to die for us because He knew that His Atonement would bring great joy and healing!

[Hebrews 12:1–2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/heb/12?lang=eng&id=p1-p2#p1)
