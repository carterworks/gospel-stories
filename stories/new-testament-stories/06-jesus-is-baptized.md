Setting an example of obedience to God

[Matthew 3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng)

---

![An illustration of John the Baptist.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/mg03vcu52kncyqw8ossjqqf022m4p0o2evf2gl9e/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

God called John to help people prepare for Jesus Christ to come. John taught people to repent and be baptized. People called him John the Baptist.

[Matthew 3:1–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng&id=p1-p12#p1)

---

![Jesus coming to John to be baptized.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/luc95qd1lxbuxha356rzfl918p2ecs3v9dx8j71f/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John was a powerful teacher. Many people came to hear him and be baptized. One day, Jesus came to be baptized.

[Matthew 3:5, 13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng&id=p5,p13#p5)

---

![Jesus and John talking together.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/cdlwnr04zh27o3kitijd7zj6ow4jj6ydmmgs0d70/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

John knew that Jesus always obeyed God. He didn’t need to repent. John didn’t think he should baptize Jesus. John thought Jesus should baptize him!

[Matthew 3:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng&id=p14#p14)

---

![John baptizing Jesus.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/gfmp51prg6uxb4rhh64prp1nx6l2xqdcltlut0l2/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus said that He also needed to be baptized. He wanted to obey all His Father’s commandments. He also wanted to set an example for us. John baptized Him.

[Matthew 3:13–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng&id=p13-p15#p13); [2 Nephi 31:5–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/31?lang=eng&id=p5-p7#p5)

---

![Jesus is baptized. The Holy Ghost descends.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/yfbgvurubn2q8r9nt73sl1mf4qdimmeygguly79u/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After Jesus came out of the water, the Holy Ghost came down on Him like a dove. Heavenly Father spoke from heaven, saying, “This is my beloved Son.” Heavenly Father was very happy with Jesus.

[Matthew 3:16–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/3?lang=eng&id=p16-p17#p16)
