Paul’s lesson from a “thorn”

[2 Corinthians 12:7–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/2-cor/12?lang=eng&id=p7-p10#p7)

---

![The Apostle Paul teaching people about the Savior.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ls0mcqi6rnbveyahairth8d6h99ooysnhg6iku1m/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Apostle Paul was a great servant of Jesus Christ. He had many revelations and taught people about the Savior. But Paul also had many challenges.

[2 Corinthians 12:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/2-cor/12?lang=eng&id=p7#p7)

---

![Paul writing a letter to Church members in Corinth.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0exo3h4c85q6ffkte9c3xpogsvo750dv7yssitb1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In a letter to Church members, Paul compared his challenges to having a thorn stuck in his skin. It made Paul feel very weak.

[2 Corinthians 12:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/2-cor/12?lang=eng&id=p7#p7)

---

![Paul praying.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/wmeua1sxy55m80z7oawv6gd6uca2spo33l1rbxl7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Three times, Paul prayed and asked the Lord to take away his challenge. But the Lord did not take it away.

[2 Corinthians 12:8–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/2-cor/12?lang=eng&id=p8-p9#p8)

---

![The Savior holding Paul’s hands and lifting him up.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fz62vqzwrg9sf7quwk8m4k39u9c15ks5zrik3w12/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Instead, the Lord told Paul that He had enough power to make Paul strong. Paul learned that sometimes a challenge can be a good thing if we turn to the Lord and ask for help. When we feel weak, Jesus Christ can make us strong, even if our challenges don’t go away.

[2 Corinthians 12:9–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/2-cor/12?lang=eng&id=p9-p10#p9)
