![People ask Jesus to bless their children.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fog4dxhk11joclh54zqtnlhrt1w68mrrxavbjgx9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

While Jesus was teaching, some people brought their young children to Him. They wanted Jesus to bless their children. His disciples tried to stop them.

[Matthew 19:13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/19?lang=eng&id=p13#p13); [Mark 10:13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/10?lang=eng&id=p13#p13)

---

![Jesus teaches His disciples to be like little children.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fos13y3ga0eizi7tmcazlf98mcw8t31bq5q466gv/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus told His disciples to let the children come to Him. He said that little children belong in the kingdom of God. He explained that if someone wants to live with God again, they need to become like a little child.

[Matthew 19:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/19?lang=eng&id=p14#p14); [Mark 10:14–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/10?lang=eng&id=p14-p15#p14)

---

![Jesus blesses the children.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/lx1xuu6cil554acw6dntr9pl7v7pe4nrf7uls6jw/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Jesus took the little children in His arms and blessed them.

[Matthew 19:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/19?lang=eng&id=p15#p15); [Mark 10:16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/10?lang=eng&id=p16#p16)
