Loving our neighbors as we love ourselves

[Luke 10:25–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p25-p37#p25)

---

![A man asks Jesus a question.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ld47nc0glqa67jbowyd5d6sk3zjyohfyujr0q6lv/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A lawyer asked Jesus how to receive eternal life. Jesus asked him what was written in the scriptures. The lawyer answered that the scriptures say to love God and love our neighbor. Jesus said he was right. Then the lawyer asked, “Who is my neighbor?” Jesus answered by telling a parable or story.

[Luke 10:25–29](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p25-p29#p25)

---

![A Jewish man is injured by thieves.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/58we7oykwqpjoblpioqzto699uken3nv12e67k6i/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In the parable, a man who was a Jew was traveling on the road from Jerusalem to Jericho. Suddenly, some thieves came. They stole his clothes, hurt him badly, and left him there.

[Luke 10:30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p30#p30)

---

![A priest ignores the injured man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/540i8mffa52boevlzwtir633c4py9gktniy9tf0a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Soon, a priest came down the road. A priest was a person who worked in the temple. He saw the man on the road but passed by on the other side.

[Luke 10:31](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p31#p31)

---

![A Levite ignores the injured man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/tgmcyx1gcqbvu4qyr3gq0a2wzoh679zoae4k4e09/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Next came another man who also worked in the temple, called a Levite. He saw the man who was hurt, but he passed by on the other side of the road too.

[Luke 10:32](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p32#p32)

---

![A Samaritan notices the Jewish man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0jwtsfscovu6gqt3i34v4lfwd9pp2jxghxhv4oel/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then a Samaritan walked by. He saw that the man on the road was hurt very badly. Samaritans and Jews usually did not get along well. But this Samaritan wanted to help the man anyway.

[Luke 10:33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p33#p33)

---

![The Samaritan takes care of the Jewish man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/lod76r6mv641ik9gkgh0b7oer63woglwy3y1uput/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Samaritan cared for the man by putting oil and wine on the man’s wounds. He put the man on his donkey.

[Luke 10:34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p34#p34)

---

![The Samaritan takes the Jewish man to an inn.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/nsuuzriapq22xrolxm0aa2q2b2ovw0lti9xx3rgb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then the Samaritan brought the man to an inn, where he could rest and get better. Before the Samaritan left the next day, he gave the innkeeper money and said, “Take care of him.” The Samaritan said he would be back in a few days with more money.

[Luke 10:34–35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p34-p35#p34)

---

![Jesus tells the lawyer to be like the Samaritan.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/wv5wqj13exvx12heya85vsizx769g3vn3m6k2gu9/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

At the end of the story, Jesus asked the lawyer a question. Who was the hurt man’s neighbor? The priest, the Levite, or the Samaritan? The lawyer said it was the one who had shown love to the man—the Samaritan. Jesus told the lawyer to go and be like the Samaritan.

[Luke 10:36–37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/10?lang=eng&id=p36-p37#p36)
