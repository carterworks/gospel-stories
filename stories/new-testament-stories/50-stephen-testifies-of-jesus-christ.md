A courageous servant gives his life for his testimony

[Acts 6–7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/6?lang=eng)

---

![Two Apostles with members of Jesus’s Church.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/nlr9rnynrdgleixwxx27rcyfppvo1moip4au3vu5/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Church was growing quickly. It was getting harder for the Apostles to take care of all the members.

[Acts 6:1–2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/6?lang=eng&id=p1-p2#p1)

---

![An Apostle gives Stephen a priesthood blessing.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/1l05bvozpftw92lyr8v10holqm14qiu15id9xdh3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The Apostles decided to call seven men who could help. They asked the members to look for men who were wise and full of the Holy Ghost.

[Acts 6:3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/6?lang=eng&id=p3#p3)

---

![Stephen healing a man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/iiee9a8v5jqik74tycv0iek5u4apowmsjj7biwdg/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One of the people they chose was Stephen. The Apostles gave him a blessing. Stephen had a lot of faith and did great miracles among the people.

[Acts 6:5–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/6?lang=eng&id=p5-p8#p5)

---

![Stephen talking to the high priest and other leaders.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/oav21a438jb5gbuhrjmb7v24xz21aur18s06rwdp/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Stephen taught about Jesus Christ with wisdom and with the Spirit. But the leaders of a synagogue didn’t like what he was teaching. They said he was speaking against Moses and against God. They brought Stephen before the high priest and other leaders.

[Acts 6:9–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/6?lang=eng&id=p9-p15#p9); [7:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/7?lang=eng&id=p1#p1)

---

![Stephen teaching the leaders about Jesus.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/qtv6mlmgm3bal11dqb80cp03pvpujna739xqi1ah/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Stephen’s face looked like an angel’s face. He reminded the leaders that God sent Moses to save the Israelites, but they didn’t always follow Moses. Stephen said that Moses was like Jesus Christ. God sent Jesus to save His people, but the leaders didn’t follow Him. The leaders were angry at Stephen.

[Acts 7:20–43, 51–54](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/7?lang=eng&id=p20-p43,p51-p54#p20)

---

![Stephen has a vision of Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/h58yz5ibto890s1qqc7o6dm8pcik6k79g51kb3ax/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Filled with the Holy Ghost, Stephen looked into heaven. He saw Jesus Christ standing next to Heavenly Father.

[Acts 7:55–56](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/7?lang=eng&id=p55-p56#p55)

---

![The leaders grab Stephen.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/mzrlwtbid8go4gy6x185y2ajybwszaa6yy7hgvlw/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Stephen told the leaders what he saw, they became even more upset. They shouted in anger and grabbed Stephen. Then they dragged him out of the city.

[Acts 7:57](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/7?lang=eng&id=p57#p57)

---

![Saul watching the death of Stephen.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fd51w77gr07hulak70jphh5py0tbh6xim2rab7el/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

They laid their coats at the feet of a man named Saul. Then they threw stones at Stephen to put him to death. Saul agreed with what they were doing. Stephen prayed. He asked the Lord to forgive the people who were hurting him. He also asked the Lord to receive his spirit into heaven.

[Acts 7:59–60](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/7?lang=eng&id=p59-p60#p59); [8:1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/acts/8?lang=eng&id=p1#p1)
