A sacrifice to save us from sin and death

[Matthew 27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng); [Luke 23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/23?lang=eng); [John 19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/19?lang=eng)

---

![Soldiers put a crown of thorns on Jesus’s head.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/y89oyx7n7yu0ozz6v0dm291xrql95hd63uzn56d7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After Pilate decided that Jesus should be crucified, he told his soldiers to take Jesus away. They whipped Him, spit on Him, put a crown of thorns on His head, and made fun of Him.

[Mark 15:15–20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/15?lang=eng&id=p15-p20#p15)

---

![Jesus carries a heavy wooden cross.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/yw4yiu8awifux6gcnoadxlb5zysbwcxbt8je4amq/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The soldiers made Jesus carry a heavy wooden cross. They took Him to a place just outside Jerusalem called Golgotha.

[John 19:17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/19?lang=eng&id=p17#p17)

---

![The soldiers nail Jesus to the cross.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/kkw7chlpc386n5f3mzrcn2eau3mjprwf0l2a09j3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The soldiers nailed Jesus’s hands and feet to the cross. Jesus asked His Heavenly Father to forgive the soldiers, because they didn’t know they were crucifying the Son of God.

[Luke 23:33–34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/23?lang=eng&id=p33-p34#p33); see also the Joseph Smith Translation in footnote 34 *c*

---

![A crowd looks at Jesus and two criminals being crucified.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/p63slj033ephypfn2ynvoho614hcomwr992rj5ch/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Two criminals were crucified with Jesus that day. One of them said to Him, “If thou be the Christ, save thyself and us.”

[Luke 23:39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/23?lang=eng&id=p39#p39)

---

![Jesus talks with one of the criminals.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4iwhxwfuvlvh5m281m75vo1q13c1jbwkuoaoery1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The other criminal said they deserved their punishment, but Jesus had done nothing wrong. He asked Jesus to remember him in His kingdom. Jesus told him that soon they would be together in the spirit world.

[Luke 23:40–43](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/23?lang=eng&id=p40-p43#p40)

---

![Jesus tells John to take care of his mother.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/7wpedzot7i302erknyjz8rz1xodl0m3g0s2rlqtr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Many women who followed Jesus Christ had come to the cross to be with Him. One of the women was Jesus’s mother, Mary. Jesus saw her and told John, one of His Apostles, to take care of her.

[John 19:25–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/19?lang=eng&id=p25-p27#p25); see also [Luke 8:1–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/8?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1); [Mark 15:40–41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/15?lang=eng&id=p40-p41#p40)

---

![Darkness covers the land.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/libcwpebtum8w9u036b1i6mkhm09ivmo10m064oi/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

For three hours, darkness covered the land. Jesus felt very lonely. He felt like His Father in Heaven had left Him.

[Matthew 27:45–46](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng&id=p45-p46#p45)

---

![Jesus gives up His life.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/j0003wtof97zvq9hio74qz8l198hy2q2acsvqut7/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Finally, Jesus knew that He had completed His suffering. He said, “Father, it is finished, thy will is done.” Then Jesus bowed His head and gave up His life. To see if He was really dead, a soldier pierced Him in the side with a spear.

[Joseph Smith Translation, Matthew 27:54](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/jst/jst-matt/27?lang=eng&id=p54#p54) (in [Matthew 27:50](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng&id=p50#p50), footnote *a*); [John 19:28–30, 34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/19?lang=eng&id=p28-p30,p34#p28)

---

![The veil in the temple tears in half.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/g8mymgi0fuwxy72pwll06n3bzvhz3a89imensgnb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When Jesus died, the earth shook. Rocks broke into pieces. The veil of the temple, the curtain covering the holiest place in the temple, tore in half.

[Matthew 27:51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng&id=p51#p51)

---

![The soldiers watching Jesus are afraid.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4zxizbi06g6p5658d8n791sfhadzbw3r0dq9i3em/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The soldiers were afraid. “Truly this was the Son of God,” they said.

[Matthew 27:54](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng&id=p54#p54)

---

![Jesus’s mother and His disciples place His body in a tomb.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/euvvrw31p8f951oxm9tbwmfkgx6axjh58kztsrq1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus’s disciples wrapped His body in cloth and put Him in a tomb in a garden. Later, they rolled a large stone in front of the door of the tomb.

[Matthew 27:57–61](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/27?lang=eng&id=p57-p61#p57); [John 19:38–41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/19?lang=eng&id=p38-p41#p38)
