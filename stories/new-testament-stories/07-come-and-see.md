Inviting a friend to follow Jesus

[John 1:43–51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p43-p51#p43)

---

![Philip listening to Jesus as He teaches people.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ac9xl39lebmg394h05ajnaez3ojtpc263h5g0cc8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

A man named Philip was one of Jesus’s followers. He knew that Jesus was the Savior. Philip wanted his friend Nathanael to know about Jesus too.

[John 1:43–45](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p43-p45#p43)

---

![Philip talking to his friend Nathanael.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/y3b6addx0xefavz1v8b2xj6sh0njjp246ah2i9k1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Nathanael knew that the scriptures said God would send a Savior. Philip told him that he had found the Savior—His name was Jesus, from Nazareth.

[John 1:45](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p45#p45)

---

![Philip inviting Nathanael to come and see Jesus.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/3fbkxd94l9eyd6b5b5rr2in6s37ueput70zdwrn3/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

At first Nathanael didn’t believe him. He didn’t think the Savior could be from Nazareth. “Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth?” he asked. Philip answered, “Come and see.”

[John 1:46](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p46#p46)

---

![Jesus greeting Nathanael.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/kzo4tx3960wdispyevjcfnxds0zg064x9tr1ukw0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Nathanael went with Philip to meet Jesus. When Jesus saw them coming, He said that Nathanael was a good and honest man. Nathanael was surprised. He had never met Jesus before. How did Jesus know him?

[John 1:47–48](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p47-p48#p47)

---

![Jesus talking with Nathanael.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/4es0p0cvwum7z0e2zxqhwcmtmnuvkr0nl84rvmx8/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus told Nathanael that before Philip talked to him, Jesus had seen him sitting under a fig tree.

[John 1:48](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p48#p48)

---

![Nathanael kneeling to Jesus.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ve57dzti0f26fqvdh3hs5m8rz9jerbmqwebses61/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Now Nathanael knew that Jesus was the Son of God. Jesus told Nathanael he would see great things—like angels coming out of heaven—all because of his faith.

[John 1:49–51](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/1?lang=eng&id=p49-p51#p49)
