A healing that happens over time

[Mark 8:22–25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/8?lang=eng&id=p22-p25#p22)

---

![People bring a blind man to be healed by Jesus.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/g0mhscprh40c7v4vaz8r465fo4c3768xg23ova10/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In a town called Bethsaida, people brought a blind man to Jesus. They wanted Jesus to bless him so he could see.

[Mark 8:22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/8?lang=eng&id=p22#p22)

---

![Jesus blesses the man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ljrn1asn3xxnf1qsbc06zxkzzhnik2sd5tc0hxip/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus held the man’s hand and led him out of the town. Then Jesus put His hands on the man and blessed him.

[Mark 8:23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/8?lang=eng&id=p23#p23)

---

![The man can now see, but not very well.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/edwdij31mzmrnqjvx5o0kiyqoi3ia0kskvtu1cmr/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus asked the man if he could see anything. The man looked up. He said he could see a little bit, but not very clearly. He said that people looked like walking trees.

[Mark 8:23–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/8?lang=eng&id=p23-p24#p23)

---

![The man can now see clearly.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/h46p6ysattkdwb61vaf4233pss6xqeujcwv71kv6/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus blessed the man again. This time, when he looked up, everything was clear. Jesus had healed him!

[Mark 8:25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/8?lang=eng&id=p25#p25)
