Jesus teaches about forgiveness

Matthew 18:21–34

---

![Peter asks Jesus a question.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ozttkzmdqohbh8pdqyxbn8gdunt8rrqru2mvzhdo/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

One day, Peter asked Jesus an important question. He wanted to know how many times he should forgive people when they sin. “Seven times?” Peter asked. Jesus answered Peter’s question by telling him a story or parable.

[Matthew 18:15–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p15-p21#p15)

---

![A servant owes money to the king.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9raaw7irw5uqbz5tmejti6n109korb9gnky3aooo/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

In the story, there was a king who had many servants. One of the servants owed the king ten thousand talents. That was a lot of money!

[Matthew 18:23–24](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p23-p24#p23)

---

![The servant is unable to pay the debt.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/0limvk2axw3zxxp4zk1sf2gi2ujcugyfs8n2jrfn/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The servant could never pay back the king. The king said that he would sell the servant and his family to pay off the debt.

[Matthew 18:25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p25#p25)

---

![The servant asks for forgiveness.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ufnazedx1ei14g0bficm3zcxmr6h2ljnh3bzgrrt/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the servant heard this, he fell to his knees. He begged the king to give him more time. “Have patience with me,” he said, “and I will pay thee all.”

[Matthew 18:26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p26#p26)

---

![The king forgives the servant.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9pbpfjx319oatkki9q5gy97y8kt46yxfwegsrtrb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The king had mercy for the servant. He decided to forgive him. The servant did not have to repay the money. He was free to go!

[Matthew 18:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

---

![The servant demands money from another man.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/oz2escqgv2k0gfwvtb8dvlkvaw2blwf6rwpb2hmk/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then the servant went and found a man who owed him a small amount of money. He grabbed the man and said, “Pay me.”

[Matthew 18:28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p28#p28)

---

![The man asks the servant for forgiveness.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/xtk4ms3lk8f1mg016j69ou03etcx21ic591inxve/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

The man fell to his knees. “Have patience with me,” he said, “and I will pay thee all.” But the servant did not forgive him. He sent him to prison.

[Matthew 18:29–30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p29-p30#p29)

---

![The king tells the servant about forgiveness.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9jpyqny1a40brq9cagjp88g86mnm3etowpjoej96/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When the king found out what had happened, he asked to see the servant. The king reminded his servant that he had been forgiven of a very large debt. He told his servant that he should have also forgiven the man who owed him money.

[Matthew 18:31–33](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p31-p33#p31)

---

![Jesus talking with Peter about forgiveness.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/fqq5jzckp3kh4hp3i55cgqg8jsbt7nnvsmfjal3c/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

We all owe a debt we could never pay for ourselves. Jesus explained to Peter that God forgives us, so we should also forgive others. And we should not stop after seven times but should always be willing to forgive.

[Matthew 18:22, 34–35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/18?lang=eng&id=p22,p34-p35#p22); see also [Matthew 6:14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/6?lang=eng&id=p14#p14); [Doctrine and Covenants 64:9–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/64?lang=eng&id=p9-p10#p9)
