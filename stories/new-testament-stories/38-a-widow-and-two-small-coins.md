Jesus teaches about sacrifice

[Mark 12:41–44](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/12?lang=eng&id=p41-p44#p41)

---

![Jesus watches people donate money at the temple.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/o86yus83xkwmlxdl16dgt3yol6iwwq3jh9txwfpu/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus went to the temple in Jerusalem. While He was there, He watched as people gave money for the work of the temple. Many rich people gave a lot of money.

[Mark 12:41](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/12?lang=eng&id=p41#p41)

---

![A poor woman’s small coins.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/uajfoenc627qvxeky14fea3y6qoe4g02q0up0nvd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then a poor widow gave two coins called mites. It was not very much, but it was all the money she had.

[Mark 12:42, 44](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/12?lang=eng&id=p42,p44#p42)

---

![The woman donates two coins.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/9fg3rfppg9p7xknz53ghqn2yflnb66ipi3l6pzs1/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus saw the widow and asked His disciples to come see what she had done.

[Mark 12:43](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/12?lang=eng&id=p43#p43)

---

![Jesus teaches about the woman’s sacrifice.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/es1cmlwqxoxg88863gr7c1n6bphjrspwdk0euc7o/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus said that the widow had given more than anyone. The rich people had given a lot of money, but it was only a small part of what they had. The widow gave only a little bit of money, but she gave all that she had.

[Mark 12:43–44](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/mark/12?lang=eng&id=p43-p44#p43)
