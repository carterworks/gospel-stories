Jesus introduces the sacrament

[Matthew 26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/26?lang=eng); [Luke 22](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng); [John 13–14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng)

---

![Jesus and His Apostles in Jerusalem.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5v3p3td9mo3u5wqgeaa9j9y5kj6o48dpd4nfv1vz/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus and His Apostles were in Jerusalem to celebrate Passover. The Jewish people celebrated Passover every year. It helped them remember that, many years ago, their families were slaves in Egypt, and God helped them escape.

[Exodus 12:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ex/12?lang=eng&id=p27#p27)

---

![Jesus and His Apostles gathered around a table.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/vdg36n9rlnhapnynsi4a8blgh5ceuvjsz7agblg0/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

While they were in Jerusalem, Jesus and His Apostles had a special feast together. Jesus had been looking forward to sharing this meal with them.

[Luke 22:7–15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng&id=p7-p15#p7)

---

![Jesus washing the foot of one of His Apostles.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e1cpcrjwusffrhc1omgxkxy9ljraz6f04em625mb/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After they finished eating, Jesus filled a bowl with water. He began to wash the feet of His Apostles.

[John 13:4–5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng&id=p4-p5#p4)

---

![Peter asking why Jesus is washing their feet.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/ab3l5qny47xj44mhg2l598k0n5v6d7tfsby9c8xa/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

When it was Peter’s turn, he asked why Jesus was doing this. Washing feet was something that usually only servants did.

[John 13:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng&id=p6#p6)

---

![Jesus teaching His Apostles about how to love one another.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/t0mslixl0hofotreodftjxt9mkk9fqjosyz9s2bp/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus said He was washing their feet to set an example for them. He wanted them to serve and love one another as He had loved them. If they did this, people would know they were His disciples.

[John 13:14–16, 34–35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng&id=p14-p16,p34-p35#p14)

---

![Peter talking with Jesus.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/b555ayxh5yxl37l2jhdb8eq4q45cpi83i6w32s10/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus told His disciples that He would die soon. Peter said he was willing to die for Jesus.

[John 13:36–38](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng&id=p36-p38#p36)

---

![Jesus responding to Peter.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/994cb5xjpdlwu6gwonmwvuj2naakyrnzgvm2d6wd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

But Jesus knew hard times were coming. He told Peter that before the rooster crowed the next morning, Peter would say three times that He didn’t know Jesus.

[John 13:38](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/13?lang=eng&id=p38#p38)

---

![Jesus teaching His Apostles about the Holy Ghost.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/c275u0r3k5qstqbcx7osb9fq9dl6wrsw5sbjqf0g/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus knew that His Apostles might be worried and afraid. He promised that God would send them the Holy Ghost. The Holy Ghost would comfort them, teach them, and help them remember what Jesus had taught them. He told them not to be worried or afraid. He said, “If ye love me, keep my commandments.”

[John 14:1, 15–16, 26–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/14?lang=eng&id=p1,p15-p16,p26-p27#p1)

---

![Jesus breaking bread for the sacrament.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/dfrin6e5pcf7bzf7tq5j1iwkcrifvvat13kbh89a/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Jesus did something very special to help His Apostles always remember Him. He gave them the sacrament. First, He blessed bread, broke it into pieces, and gave it to His Apostles. Jesus told them to think of His body when they ate the bread. He asked them to remember that He would die for them.

[Matthew 26:26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/26?lang=eng&id=p26#p26); [Luke 22:19](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng&id=p19#p19)

---

![Jesus blessing the wine for the sacrament.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/iyoea11dta2z5xoxndpq79qxc1d423ntrorrwb42/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Jesus then poured wine into a cup. He blessed the wine and told the Apostles to drink it. Jesus told them to think of His blood when they drank the wine. He asked them to remember that He would suffer, bleed, and die for the pain and sins of all people so we could repent and be forgiven.

[Matthew 26:27–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/26?lang=eng&id=p27-p28#p27); [Luke 22:20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/luke/22?lang=eng&id=p20#p20)

---

![Jesus teaching His Apostles to keep His commandments.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/5qbphoi41wj90fzxfaxo1vc7lqjcm7yk2n079u3y/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

Then Jesus explained that He is like a vine, and His disciples are like branches. If a branch stays connected to the vine, it grows fruit. A branch that is not connected to the vine dies and can’t grow fruit. Jesus wanted His disciples to always stay close to Him by loving one another and keeping His commandments.

[John 15:4–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/15?lang=eng&id=p4-p12#p4)

---

![Jesus and His Apostles walking to Gethsemane.](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/imgs/e9j06560dvt6528epntc2odkcj01hm9kwdljmhzd/full/%21640%2C/0/default)

After teaching these things, Jesus and His Apostles sang a hymn. They walked toward a garden called Gethsemane.

[Matthew 26:30](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/26?lang=eng&id=p30#p30)
